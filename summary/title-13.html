Title XIII · summary

Transitional And Final Provisions

Articles 320 to 340 · 21 articles · 4 chapters · read the full text

Chapter 1 · Adoption and Entry into Force

320

Adoption

This Constitution takes its authority from a referendum of the residents.

321

Entry into force

It comes into force thirty days after the result, with some parts phased in.

322

Repeal

The constitutions of 2024 and 1890 are repealed, and preserved as history only.

323

Continuity of laws

Existing laws continue, conflicts are void at once, and the rest must conform within three years.

324

Continuity of acts, rights and proceedings

Past acts stay valid, pending cases continue, and provision is never interrupted.

Chapter 2 · Constitution of the New Organs

325

The Judicial Council

The Judicial Council is constituted first, drawn by lot and elected by sitting judges.

326

The Constitutional Court

The Constitutional Court follows within eight months, its first terms staggered by lot.

327

Judges and prosecution

Sitting judges continue, with up to six years of past service counting toward their term.

328

The oversight offices

The oversight offices are constituted within a year, with staggered first terms.

329

The Ecological Budget Authority and the first budgets

The Ecological Budget Authority is set up in six months, and publishes the first budgets in two years.

Chapter 3 · Transition of the Assembly, Execution and the Plan

330

The Assembly and the executive committees

The National Council continues until the first Assembly of two hundred is elected within a year.

331

The planning bodies and the first plan

The planning bodies are built over two years, running the inherited plan meanwhile.

332

Territories

Existing communes continue while the sub-regions and regions are established.

333

Provision, housing and devices

Existing provision levels count as the minimum core until organic law specifies it.

334

Working time and contribution

The twenty-four hour week applies at once, with two years to reorganise stubborn tasks.

335

Electronic voting

Electronic voting waits at least two years, and until the Commission certifies the conditions.

336

Union and external relations

Bloc membership continues while its founding instrument is brought into conformity.

Chapter 4 · Final Provisions

337

First distribution and summary

The summary is published within a year, and the booklet distributed within eighteen months.

338

Reservation of the amending power

Transitional provisions expire when their transitions are done, and can never be extended.

339

Authorship and method

The text was drafted by the people with machine assistance, under human decision throughout.

340

Text and languages

All language versions are equally authentic, and headings and the summary are not operative text.
