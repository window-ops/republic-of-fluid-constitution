Register › Official summary › Title III

Fundamental Rights And Duties

Articles 37 to 81 · 45 articles · 8 chapters · read the full text

Chapter 1 · General Provisions

37

Holders

Every right here belongs to every person present, documented or not.

38

Equality

Everyone is equal before the law, and discrimination on any comparable ground is prohibited.

39

Effect

These rights apply directly without any statute, and bind employers and services too.

40

Limitation

A right may be limited only by clear law, for a listed purpose, and no more than necessary.

41

Minimum core and progression

Each social right has a minimum core enforceable today, and above it plans must be reasonable.

42

Derogation

Rights may be suspended only in a declared emergency, and some of them never at all.

43

Remedies

Anyone whose rights are threatened gets a fast, free remedy and reparation.

44

Prohibition of abuse

Nothing here may be used to destroy the rights it protects.

Chapter 2 · Integrity and Liberty

45

Life

Everyone has a right to life, and the death penalty can never return.

46

Integrity and autonomy of the person

You decide about your own body, including reproduction, gender care and refusing treatment.

47

Prohibition of torture

Torture is prohibited, as is sending anyone anywhere they risk it.

48

Servitude and forced labour

Slavery, trafficking and forced labour are prohibited, including labour demanded in return for provision.

49

Liberty and security

Detention needs legal grounds and a judge within forty-eight hours, and the remedy is never suspended.

50

Private life and the home

Home, family life and messages are private, and mass surveillance can never be authorised.

51

Data and automated decision

Your data is yours, cannot be traded, and no machine may decide anything about you.

Chapter 3 · Conscience, Expression and Association

52

Thought, conscience and religion

Belief is free, including having none, and nobody may be forced to declare one.

53

Expression and information

You may say what you think, censorship is prohibited, and no opinion earns a prison term.

54

Means of communication

Communication infrastructure is socially owned, transmitted without discrimination, and never concentrated.

55

Assembly

Peaceful assembly needs no permission, and strikes and occupations are protected.

56

Association and self-organisation

You may form, join and leave any association, and unions are protected inside cooperatives.

Chapter 4 · Residence, Citizenship and Political Rights

57

Residence and movement

You may move and settle freely, and collective expulsion is prohibited.

58

Citizenship

Citizenship comes automatically after five years and adds no right a resident lacks.

59

Asylum

Asylum is a right, and irregular entry is never punished.

60

Political rights

Every resident aged sixteen votes and may stand, including people held in detention.

61

Petition, information and protection of disclosure

You may petition any body, information is public, and whistleblowers are protected.

Chapter 5 · Provision, Contribution and Social Rights

62

Guarantee of essential provision

Food, water, housing, energy, healthcare, education, care and transport are free and unconditional.

63

Registration of needs

You register what you need, and the planners are bound by what you register.

64

Food and water

Enough safe food and water is guaranteed, and hunger is a breach of the minimum core.

65

Housing

Housing is adequate and secure, eviction into homelessness is prohibited, and letting for gain is banned.

66

Health

Healthcare is available to everyone, and emergency and essential care is never refused.

67

Education

Education is free at every level, and institutions govern their own teaching and research.

68

Care

Care is a public responsibility, and unpaid care at home counts as work.

69

Energy, mobility and communication

Energy, transport and network access sufficient for public life are guaranteed.

70

Contribution

Everyone contributes according to capacity, everyone is presumed to, and no service depends on proving it.

71

Conditions of labour

Work must be safe, workers govern their enterprise, and dismissal never touches provision.

72

Working time and rest

The working week is at most twenty-four hours over four days, with rest and leave guaranteed.

73

Culture, science and the arts

Culture, science and the arts are free, and creators keep credit but no exclusive economic right.

74

Personal property and inheritance

Personal things are yours and protected, but cannot become a means of employing others.

Chapter 6 · Ecological Rights of Persons

75

Healthy environment

A healthy environment is part of the minimum core and enforceable in court.

76

Ecological participation

You have a right to ecological information and to be heard before decisions are taken.

Chapter 7 · Justice

77

Access to justice

Courts are independent, hearings are public, and legal help is free where you need it.

78

Legality

Only a law in force at the time creates an offence, and heavier penalties never apply backwards.

79

Criminal process

You are innocent until proved guilty, with counsel, silence and appeal guaranteed.

80

Penalty and restoration

Punishment aims at repair and return, and life without release or indefinite detention is prohibited.

Chapter 8 · Duties

81

Duties of the person

Everyone owes respect for others' rights, honest need registration, and defence of this text.
