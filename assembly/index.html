The chamber

The Assembly

Two hundred members, elected in twenty sub-regions, with proportionality computed across the whole territory

The chamber sitting today is the interim National Council elected on 21 September 2025, which continues under Article 330(1) until the first Assembly convenes. It has one hundred members. It constitutes no executive committees, and the offices of execution inherited from the 2024 order continue under Article 330(5) until the Assembly is elected.

How the two hundred seats are filled

Members are elected by open list in the twenty sub-regions, and proportionality is computed across the whole territory. A list's national total decides how many seats it wins, and those seats go to its strongest sub-regional lists, so the chamber matches the national result while every member comes from somewhere. There is no legal threshold: a list with votes enough for one seat gets it. See Articles 120 and 121.

What happened to the turnout rule

The 2024 text made a sixty per cent turnout a condition of a valid election. On 10 June 2025 turnout was 57.3 per cent, the election was void, and the Republic had no parliament for fourteen weeks. Article 120(7) now states that in no case does the validity of an election depend on the level of participation, and Article 140(3) says the same for referendums.

There are no ministers

Execution belongs to committees of the Assembly, composed in the Assembly's own proportions, so every list represented in the chamber is represented in every committee that is large enough. Nobody is separately elected to run anything, and the chairs of the committees form a Bureau that coordinates without commanding. See Articles 147 to 149.

Recall

The residents of a district may remove the member they elected, on a petition of five per cent and a vote in the district, at any time outside the first year and the last six months of a term. No ground need be stated. See Article 128.

Provisional apportionment of the two hundred seats

Published by the Boundary Division under Article 121(5) on the resident population recorded in the register. No district returns fewer than five members, and no district departs from the average population per seat by more than ten per cent.
