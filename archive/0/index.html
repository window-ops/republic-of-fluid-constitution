Section 1: Nature of the State

The Republic of Fluid is a sovereign, independent, unitary, and indivisible State.

The State affirms the Republic as its fundamental form of governance, based on the rule of law and democratic participation.

Section 2: Democratic and Social Character

The Republic of Fluid is a democratic and social State committed to upholding the rule of law.

It shall guarantee the inherent dignity of every individual, the protection and promotion of human rights and fundamental freedoms, and the full development of human potential.

The social function of the State encompasses justice, equality, solidarity, and political pluralism, inspired by humanist, just, and socialist traditions rooted in the nation’s heritage.

The State shall actively promote social welfare, economic equity, and access to education, healthcare, and social security for all citizens.

Section 3: Separation and Balance of Powers

The organization of the Republic of Fluid is founded upon the principle of separation of powers into three independent branches: legislative, executive, and judicial.

The independence of the judiciary shall be guaranteed to uphold justice impartially and safeguard constitutional order.

Section 4: Sovereignty of the People

Sovereignty resides unconditionally in the people of the Republic of Fluid.

The people exercise their sovereignty directly through referendums and participatory mechanisms, and indirectly through freely elected representatives.

The election of representatives shall be conducted by universal, equal, free, secret, and fair suffrage, in conformity with constitutional provisions.

No individual, institution, or group may usurp or exercise sovereign power independent of the people.

Section 5: Unity and Indivisibility of the State

The Republic of Fluid is founded upon the unity and indivisibility of its territory and people.

The Republic respects and guarantees equal rights and protections to all citizens regardless of race, nationality, ethnicity, language, religion, gender, political beliefs, social status, or any other distinction.

The State fosters social cohesion and national identity rooted in respect for diversity and equality.

Section 6: Political Pluralism and Democratic Participation

Political pluralism is an essential condition and safeguard of constitutional democracy in the Republic of Fluid.

Political parties and other civil society organizations shall freely operate and contribute to the political life of the nation, reflecting the diverse opinions and interests of the citizenry.

All political activities shall respect the principles of national sovereignty, territorial integrity, the rule of law, and democratic governance.

The State shall ensure the conditions for transparency, accountability, and inclusiveness in political processes.
