Republic of Fluid Constitution

About

The Republic of Fluid is a fictional democratic socialist republic that has achieved a society that has very little class differences, is mostly moneyless, and is characterized by its high quality of life, robust social cohesion, and a strong commitment to scientific and technological progress. Below is its constitution, organized in a list. Please note that this is a simplified constitution, and all the content may not be entirely realistic (it's a bit utopian, and there is no realistic class struggle narrative).

Preamble

In order to ensure the well-being of all citizens, the people of The Republic of Fluid hereby establish this Constitution. We strive for a society that values each person's inherent worth, treats all with fairness and compassion, and ensure a fair distribution of resources.
