How the search works

The words are matched as one continuous phrase. When that finds nothing, the words are matched again in any order. Double quotes search the exact phrase and suppress the second pass. A number on its own opens that article.

Filters can be typed as well as clicked: in:constitution, in:summary, in:history, in:concepts, in:economy, in:questions, in:assembly, in:map, in:world, in:about, title:5, chapter:2, art:100-140, period:directorate. Filters of the same kind widen the search, filters of different kinds narrow it. The title, chapter, article and period filters apply to the constitution, the summary and the history, and selecting one of them sets the register pages aside.

The searched text sits in js/search-data.js, a flattened copy of text/constitution.md, text/summary.md and text/history.md, together with the concepts, economy, questions, assembly, map and about pages.
