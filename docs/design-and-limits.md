# Design guarantees and open questions

# Protection against a debt and conditionality crisis

The design goal recorded in `questions/index.html` is that the mechanism which produced the Greek adjustment programmes cannot be reached from inside the Republic's own institutions.

The reasoning given by the Chair of the Committee for the Economy is stated narrowly. A government that has borrowed abroad under conditionality has stopped deciding its own budget, whatever its constitution says and however its people vote, so removing the mechanism is the more reliable course.

| Provision | Effect |
| - | - |
| Article 117(5) | Borrowing abroad, external debt, pledging the common heritage or a productive asset, and external financial conditionality prohibited |
| Article 291(1) | Treaties creating any of those prohibited |
| Article 265(1)(o) | Such an act void, whoever adopts it and by whatever majority |
| Article 310(1)(j) | That nullity placed beyond the amending power, so no referendum restores the capacity |
| Article 295(5) | A common currency, a common monetary authority, and any obligation to maintain a rate, a reserve or a convertibility refused |
| Article 294 | Union acts denied direct effect, so no external body legislates here without the Assembly |
| Articles 249 to 251 | Creditor scrutiny replaced by audit in physical and ecological units, published in full, openable by ten thousand residents |

# What the design leaves open

`economy/unresolved.html` publishes seven questions the Republic cannot answer, since Article 318 will ask about them in 2036. The most consequential for daily life is shortage.

The text supplies a lawful procedure for allocating scarcity and no guarantee against scarcity itself. Article 113 governs how claims are ranked when provision falls short, and Article 114 voids the parallel markets that ranked them informally through the winter of 2024. Neither prevents the shortage.

Kornai's account of the soft budget constraint is answered in `economy/objections.html` with a published horizon. Article 100 gives every enterprise a binding resource ceiling and public physical accounts, Article 102 caps support at two years renewable once with the deciders named, and expiry opens reorganisation by operation of law. The concession recorded on the same page is that the cap sits in organic law, which three fifths of the chamber can loosen.

Four related uncertainties are published alongside it:

- Whether voluntary labour reaches night sanitation, care of the violently ill and deep maintenance, sustained over decades
- Whether the negotiated cycle scales beyond eighteen million people and one language of administration
- Whether the residual unit stays residual once it meets a real shortage, having been written after the last one
- Whether external dependence falls, given entries in pharmaceutical precursors and semiconductor fabrication with no domestic answer at any price the ecological budgets allow
