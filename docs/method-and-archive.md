# Method and the archive

# Method and the use of machine assistance

The author is not a lawyer, and most of the raw text was produced with machine assistance.

The workflow put questions back to the author whenever a drafting choice was legally difficult: the effect of unconstitutionality, the reach of the unamendable core, the standard of review for social rights, the treatment of foreign economic entities, the allocation rule for scarcity. Each was decided by the author and recorded in the standing decisions table at the head of `text/constitution.md`, which lists 47 such decisions with the option taken.

Article 339 records the same fact in the world of the text. The Constitution was drafted through deliberative bodies in part with the assistance of reasoning systems, under human direction and human decision at every stage.

# The archive

`/archive` holds the 2024 programme and the 1890 text in their original form, including the map, flag, parliament, history and Bloc pages that accompanied the 2024 site.

Article 322(3) requires a repealed text to be kept integrally, so the archive keeps its original stylesheet and its external resources. It is the one part of this repository that reaches outside it.

Article 322(4) governs the standing of the archived texts. They are citable as history and as evidence of the intention of their makers, never as authority, and are applied by no court and no organ.
