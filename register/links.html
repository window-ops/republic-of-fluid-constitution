The sites of the organs constituted by the Constitution, and of the licensed bodies outside them

This register publishes the Constitution. The organs constituted by it publish their own work, each on its own site, under Article 242(1). The directory of those sites is maintained by the Office of Constitutional Revision under Article 317. Where a page of any of them differs from the text of the Constitution, the text governs, and Article 315(4) gives any person the right to report the divergence and receive a reasoned answer.

Foreign entities licensed under Article 21

Eleven entities constituted under foreign law hold a revocable licence under Article 21, granted for a term and withdrawable before it. They own nothing in the Republic, hold no right under Title III, can take no public function, and can compel arbitration in no case. Each is required to publish the conditions it holds its licence upon.
