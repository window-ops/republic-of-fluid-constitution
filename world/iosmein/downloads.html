Downloads

Every file this portal publishes, sorted by what it contains

The files have no licence condition and need no registration and no account. Article 17(3) gives every person access to the documents held by an organ, and Article 208(1) requires the consolidated text to be published. This Ministry keeps no record of who takes a file.

Constitution and text

constitution.mdThe consolidated text: the preamble, 217 articles in thirteen titles, the Schedule of the twelve Amendments and the Annex of displaced wording. summary.txtOne line for each of the 217 articles, grouped by title. The summary has no legal force, and the Constitution governs where the two differ.

Terms

These files may be copied, printed, translated and republished by any person. The Ministry asks only that the date of the version be stated, since the consolidated text changes whenever a revision enters into force under Article 208(1).
