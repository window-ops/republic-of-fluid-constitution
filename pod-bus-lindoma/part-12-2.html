Two days of travelling§

The ferry to Sluland takes most of the morning. Zecina sits beside the port, and the seat costs him nothing, even though Aporia keeps a currency and prices almost everything else in it. He sleeps through most of the flight and lands at Haneda on the second afternoon. He goes through immigration, the belt, customs, and then the hall opening out ahead of him.

The hologram panel§

Vlad

pulling out a spec sheet When they issued the card, they also sent me a copy of the spec sheet, and the hologram is pretty interesting, because it's hard to fake but at the same time the architecture is open.

He puts the sheet on the table between them, one page, printed on both sides, and turns it so the hologram panel catches the light off the window.

SPEC SHEET

Tuby PowerCard, revision 6 - visual hologram panel

/src/boot/init.asm

lis     r1, 0x0010
ori     r1, r1, 0x7FF0
li      r0, 0
mtspr   1008, r0
bl      __clear_bss

issued at commit 7a4c1d9

Nozaki

turning the page a little toward herself and tilting it So the head is the file, the middle is the first five lines of what is on the card, and the bottom is which version was on it the day they handed it to you. Why put code where anyone can look at it?

Vlad

Because this card is not proprietary, so you can't hide stuff behind walls. But what they can do is to make it impossible to reproduce a card both digitally and physically without the proper infrastructure.

He turns the sheet over to the side with the checking instructions on it and puts it down where they can both reach it.

SPEC SHEET

Checking a card without a terminal

Hold the panel against a light and turn it until the three fields separate. All three must be present. A panel with no head, footer or body is not a valid card.

  • The path: the head reads /src/boot/init.asm. Any other path, or a path with no leading slash, is a forgery.
  • The body: the five lines are the opening of that file and must match the file at src.tuby.fl/ppcard character for character, spacing included. The instructions are PowerPC: mnemonics of the lis, ori, mtspr and bl families, registers written r0 to r31, special registers by number and never by name. An instruction that does not appear in the PowerPC set, or a register written in another form, means the panel was set by working from a picture.
  • The commit: the footer carries the commit the card was built from. It must resolve at src.tuby.fl/ppcard to a commit dated on or before the issue date on the reverse. A commit that does not resolve is a forgery, and so is one dated after the card.
  • The lettering: every card is printed in a face perturbed by a seed held with the issue record. The perturbation is our own form of Perlin noise and the generator is published with the rest of the software. Publication does not help a copyist: the seed of an existing card is not recoverable from the printing, and a card printed from a seed that is not in the register fails on presentation. Two cards with identical lettering are two forgeries, or one forgery and one card.

The panel is laid into the substrate. Lifting it, scraping it or heating it destroys the field it sits in, and a panel that has been worked on shows as a flat grey patch under the light.

Nozaki

Vlad

In Fluidian, when I want to say that a document is secret, I say "Et es un documenta secret", where et means "this", es is "is", un is the undefined article, and documenta is base form for "document".

Nozaki

writing これは秘密の書類です under the other two and pushing the napkin across Kore wa himitsu no shorui desu. (This is a secret document.) Kore is this, wa marks it as what I am talking about, himitsu is secret, no ties it to shorui, which is the document, and desu closes it. Your article has nowhere to go, since we have none.

Vlad

Nozaki

writing 彼らが腐敗しているので、それは秘密です Karera ga fuhai shite iru node, sore wa himitsu desu. Because they are corrupt, that is secret. The reason goes first and the thing being explained comes after, so the halves of your sentence swap places.

Vlad

Et es un documenta secret perque els es xuclar. It is a secret document because they are corrupt. This is the fused sentence.

Nozaki

writing 彼らが腐敗しているので、これは秘密の書類です Karera ga fuhai shite iru node, kore wa himitsu no shorui desu. Because they are corrupt, this is a secret document. The reason still sits first.

Vlad

Vlad

Nozaki

The procurement failures§

Vlad

PSD Direct connect would only work in Romania because of extreme deficiencies in the procurement system that are relatively easy to fix.

Nozaki

Vlad

The very corrupt politicians that benefit from those loopholes. And although the interim president is not corrupt, he does not want to concede by cutting his own pay or those of other high-ranking officials, and instead, he is cutting into the lowest-paid populations and the public sector.

Nozaki

We had a run of that, cuts to everything except the people announcing them.

Vlad

And not only that, the current interim president also has a very aggressive voice and seems to be complacent the months of rule without a full government.

Nozaki

How long has he been sitting there without a government behind him?

Vlad

More than a hundred days.

Nozaki

Here that would have ended in a month, since the party would have replaced him to stop the damage showing.

Vlad

How, because there is no stable coalition that can be formed anymore, and at the same time, if anticipatory elections occur, then extremist takeover is almost guaranteed.

Nozaki

Then the ones in power stay by arguing that whatever follows them is worse.

Vlad

Yes, and eventually, democracy ceases as those supposed pro-European moderates try to suppress their own critics. The justice system is internally polarized. On one side there are the reformists and on the other there are the counter-reformists. The reformists want to tighten the prescription system and makes sure that corrupt people actually get convicted while the counter-reformists want to attack the press that exposed the justice system as being compromised by arguing that nothing happened and that you are not allowed to say something happened.

Nozaki

So the second side is not defending the courts, it is defending the silence about them. What happens to a journalist who publishes anyway?

Vlad

They are put into a blacklist of independent press agencies that "discredited" the justice system and "threatened" democracy. They are not legally allowed to ban those agencies from reporting on the justice system.

Nozaki

A list with no power behind it can work. pushing the napkins into a pile Does anything happen to the ones on it, or is the list the whole punishment?

Vlad

Right now the list is the only punishment, but I suspect plans are brewing within the counter-reformist faction to push out the reformists and press.

Nozaki

standing and picking up her bag We should walk to the gate, and you can tell me the rest of it on the way.

Vlad

also standing up and picking up his bag Yeah, we should.
