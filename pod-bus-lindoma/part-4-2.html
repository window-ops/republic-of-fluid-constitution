The Suica at the gate§

A few days have passed since the first half of part 4. The 28 ends its inland run at the main station, and the concourse at this hour is wide and half lit, with the departure board turning over in long slow flips and the intercity gates standing open in a row at the far end. Vlad walks toward them. Akima has her wallet in her hand before she has thought about it, and taps a card against the reader out of fifteen years of habit. The gate holds, a soft double tone, and the panel fills with text.

Akima

pulling her hand back as though the reader were hot I am so sorry, I did not think. reading the panel It knows what the card is.

Vlad

Let me read it.

GATE 7

Statiun Centrala, Lindoma - intercity gate 7
Passage refused

1. Card presented

FeliCa transit card. Issuer JR East. Product name Suica. The card is intact and its balance is readable. It contains no Bloc transport record and no Bloc identifier.

2. Where this card is accepted

The ten mutually accepted networks of Japan: Suica, PASMO, ICOCA, TOICA, manaca, PiTaPa, Kitaca, SUGOCA, nimoca, hayakaken. Rail, bus and tram operators inside those networks, and retail payment at participating shops and machines. It is accepted at no gate in the Democratic Socialist Bloc.

3. Why passage is refused

Urban transport in the Republic has no fare and no gate. This gate stands on the intercity platforms, and it exists to record that a journey was made, so the operating cooperatives can predict the demand of each line. Nothing is charged and no balance is read. The record is taken here and here only. No card and no ticket is inspected aboard the train.

4. What is required

A Universal Bloc Transport Card, UBTC, or a Universal Ticket, which is the paper form of the same record and covers one journey. Both are issued without charge at the counter behind this gate and at every machine on the concourse. The card bears the hologram and the image of the building of the Ministry of Transportation of Reformed Luces Aporia at Dubrazec, where the standard was signed.

5. 日本の交通系ICカードをお持ちの方へ

このカードで当駅の改札を通ることはできません。当共和国の都市交通は無料で、改札もありません。都市間列車をご利用の場合は、統一ブロック交通カード（UBTC）または統一乗車券が必要です。いずれも無料で、この改札の後ろの窓口および構内の券売機で発行いたします。運賃はかかりません。改札での記録は各路線の需要を把握するためのものです。車内での検札はありません。

統一ブロック交通カード - 発行

カード UUID: 04:9C:2E:7B:41:80:00

1. カードについて

このカードは無料です。運賃はかかりません。改札での記録は各路線の需要を把握するためのものです。

2. EEPROM 領域（8 KB のうち 7.6 KB が空き）

交通記録以外の任意のデータを書き込むことができます。書き込まれたデータは実行されません。読み取りのみです。

  • ［緊急連絡先を書き込む］ 事故の際に駅員および救急隊が読み取ります
  • ［医療情報を書き込む］ 血液型、アレルギー、常用薬
  • ［他のカードを統合する］ 図書館カード、工具貸出カード、協同組合の組合員証
  • ［自由記述領域］ 任意のテキスト、最大 4 KB
  • ［領域を読み出す・消去する］

注意: 書き込んだ内容は当共和国内のどの端末からでも読み取り可能です。秘密にすべき事項は書き込まないでください。

Emergency contact, medical information, merging other cards into it, and four kilobytes of anything I like. And a warning that whatever I write, any terminal in the country can read.

2. EEPROM area (7.6 KB free of 8 KB)

Data of your own choosing may be written here alongside the transport record. Written data is never executed. It is read only.

  • [Write an emergency contact] Read by station staff and by ambulance crews in the event of an accident

Suica, JR East, STB, CFR and the Japanese and Romanian networks named on the panels are real, and no screen text printed here was written by them.

Akima explains gaman and going along with the group as a woman who left, and Vlad reads the Romanian railway off his own journeys. Both speak out of one life, and neither account settles what either country is.

Gaman and going along with the group are words Akima uses about herself before she uses them about anyone else, learned in her own family and her own section. Many Japanese would refuse both descriptions, and none of those people is in this carriage.
