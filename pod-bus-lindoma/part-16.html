Coming in late§

He changes at Corcina onto the coast train and goes north, then waits at Marendo for the connection further north. It is dark after Prevessa. The train comes in slowly under the canopy at Leriduson, and the platform is half lit and almost empty. The arrivals gate is open at the far end with one person standing under it.

Marliene

coming down the platform and putting her arms around him without slowing, then letting go and taking the bag off his shoulder You have been on a train for almost two days. turning and starting to walk him toward the gate Have you eaten anything since Marendo, and how much time have you got before the Torvena one?

Vlad

Yes, at Prevessa, and I have all the time in the world.

Marliene

Prevessa was an hour and a half ago. stopping under the gate and setting his bag down If you are not going west tonight, the couch at mine is free and it is eight minutes from here. Do you want to eat first, or go up and eat there?

Vlad

You choose.

Marliene

picking the bag up again and going out through the gate Then we eat here. There is a place across from the station that stays open for the night shift at the yard, and if we go up first you will sit down and not get back off the couch.

Vlad

going out through the gate alongside her Maybe, I never have been in your flat.

Marliene

You have not, and there is not much to see. crossing the road with the bag still on her shoulder It's just two rooms and a kitchen.

Vlad

It's nice that you are picking up my bag.

Marliene

You have been carrying it since Zecina.

Who Linda is§

Vlad

Yeah, and I met this woman, Linda, who was working for a mobile offshore datacenter, and I helped her file a suit against it under my name.

Marliene

stopping on the pavement outside the place Who is she exactly, where did you meet her, and why was it your name and not hers?

Vlad

I met her on the train from Cortius to Corcina. She asked me if I have seen that building off the Belvacius coast and I said that yes, I have seen the building, although I didn't see it arrive because I was out in Japan. She works for HR there, much of the information about the building is classified because of the ecological harm, and if she was found to be suing the company for the harm, she could have been permanently stuck in Fluid. She comes from the UK.

Marliene

going in and taking the table by the window So a woman you met that morning told you what she is not allowed to tell anyone, and you put your name on it before the train got to Corcina. How much of what she said could you check yourself?

Vlad

going in last and sitting alongside her Not much, that is why when I made the suit with her on the DSB section of the government's website, we wrote that we couldn't disclose the quantities and other stuff, only the position.

Marliene

That is honest, and it also means the court has your name and nothing else to keep. taking the menu card off the wall clip and putting it between them If the company writes back and says the whole thing is invented, who stands behind you? She will not, because standing behind you would be a thing she cannot do.

Vlad

I have her contact, we talked about what we would do, we filed both under local and international law, and in the republic's courts I know that you cannot really say the whole thing is invented if it refuses inspections and the water is getting warmer.

Marliene

Then it turns on the inspection. reading the card without picking it up Somebody has to go out there in a boat and come back with a measurement, and until that happens there is a file with your name and a story in it.

The call§

His phone goes off on the table and he answers.

Linda

I am in trouble. The court has moved overnight, it is on the register already, and the company has read it. a car passing on her side They have gone through who could have said any of it, and I am the only person who put anything in writing against the expansion, from the first month, so it is me.

Vlad

On the document it's my name in there, they couldn't possibly have said it was you.

Linda

They are not looking at the document. They are looking at who objected.

Vlad

And you objected. Can you defend yourself legally in the UK?

Linda

Not really. It is my contract they are working from, and the clause is theirs to enforce, so they suspend me first, take the pay, and I answer afterwards in front of their own people.

Vlad

The best line of defence for you would be not to leave Fluid.

Linda

I know, and I have not gone anywhere. What I need to know is whether the case can go on if they come after me, or whether it stops the day they suspend me.

Vlad

The case will go on.

Linda

Then let them do it. reading something on her end There is a line in the register saying a hearing is set for the ninth and an inspection is ordered.

Vlad

I didn't expect it to go so fast. It's early, I am hanging out with Marliene.

Linda

I did not either. Go back to your friend, I only wanted to say it early.

Vlad

Right. We'll talk tomorrow, when I'm back home.

Linda

Tomorrow, then. Tell her I am sorry for the interruption.

Vlad

She already heard it.

Linda

Goodnight, Vlad.

Vlad

Goodnight, Linda.

No salmon on this coast§

Marliene

waiting until he puts the phone down Is she safe where she is?

Vlad

Yes, she is safe, it's just not safe for her to go back on the platform.

Marliene

She was not going back anyway. the woman coming over to the table Order something, you have been talking since we sat down.

Vlad

Some hot salmon with eggs.

Marliene

to the woman Un pesce calid con ovu, e un supa. (One warm fish with egg, and a soup.) There is no salmon on this coast. What comes off the boats here is what you get.

Vlad

In Lindoma you could eat any type of fish.

Marliene

In Lindoma you can eat anything, because everything is brought in. sitting back Here the port largely only sends things out.

What happened after he left§

Vlad

Right, so, is there anything new going on in your life?

Marliene

Nothing. The same office, the same boats, the same rain. turning her glass on the table What happened after you left, what did you do with Nozaki?

Vlad

We left early from Satomi's house, so we could get closer to each other, and then we didn't discuss many personal things until we got to a restaurant in Tokyo. We eventually hugged each other in the Inokashira park.

Marliene

Is that where it stopped, or is there something you have not said yet?

Vlad

After that, we've gone to the hotel and then she led me to the airport and I left.

Marliene

Did the two of you say anything about what comes next, or did you both leave it where it was?

Vlad

We both left where it was.

The woman brings the plates over and sets them down without saying anything. They eat. Outside the window a yard truck goes past toward the gate, and the shift is changing somewhere behind the sheds. Neither of them says much for a while.

Feelings that lapse§

Marliene

putting her fork down We have run out of things to say.

Vlad

I don't often watch movies, and I repeat the same things over and over to different people, and I am also afraid of making things dramatic.

Marliene

You were interested in me on the branch line, and some time after that it went. I noticed, and then it happened to me as well. What a person feels in the moment lapses after some time.

Vlad

I honestly don't know what to do with that many relationships. Now I want to talk to Linda because I am interested in her, and other than that, I am not sure.

Marliene

You are interested in her because you filed something with her and it felt like it mattered. pushing her plate away Wait until the case is over and see whether you still want to phone her when there is nothing to file.

Vlad

How to choose my people?

Marliene

You do not choose them. You keep answering the ones who keep answering, and after a year you look at who is still there.

Vlad

I hope you'll still be there in the future.

Marliene

I will be. In fact, I am the one who writes first.

Vlad

I wish I didn't have to argue with people on little things related to my anxiety after enjoying the moment.

Marliene

Which people, and what were the little things?

Vlad

Mostly with my friend back in my home country, I would apologize for things I think I did wrong without being necessary.

Marliene

Has she ever asked you for any of those apologies?

Vlad

No, of course not.

Marliene

Then they are not for her. You are apologising to get the anxiety to stop.

Vlad

You can say so.

Marliene

finishing what is left in her glass and looking at the window Do you want to walk up now, or sit here until they close?

Vlad

Let's walk up now.

Fourth floor§

She puts the plates together at the edge of the table, says something to the woman on the way past, and picks up his bag again before he can reach it. They go up through the old town, six streets of low houses with the shutters closed, and then the ground rises toward the panelcu blocks with the lights of the yard behind them.

Marliene

Fourth floor, and the lift has been out since June.

Vlad

You said there were no interesting things in your apartment.

Marliene

There are not. starting up the stairs ahead of him You will see the whole of it from the door in a few seconds.

Vlad

following her up the stairs How many rooms does your unit have?

Marliene

Two and the kitchen, I told you that at the station. stopping on the third landing to change the bag to her other shoulder It's fifty-two square metres.

Vlad

As much as my new flat.

Marliene

Then they built them off the same drawings. unlocking the door and putting his bag down inside

Citation needed§

Vlad

"Communist-style buildings are possibly one of the most fascinating buildings out there from an architectural standpoint", any architect that doesn't know what else to build.

Marliene

laughing, still with her hand on the door One of the most fascinating. taking her coat off and hanging it up One of the most, according to whom. Citation needed.

Vlad

Debate goes into the talk page of this article.

Marliene

And it sits there for six years with four people arguing about the word fascinating. going through to the kitchen and putting the light on Do you want tea, or are you going straight to sleep?

Vlad

I am going straight to sleep, but before that, tell me what the wiki is not.

Marliene

laughing from the kitchen It is not a soapbox, it is not a publisher of original thought, and it is not a place for you to say a building fascinates you.

Vlad

stepping into the bedroom after the kitchen Good, now go edit the article until tomorrow, or the article will be riddled with warnings and citation needed.

Marliene

from the kitchen, still laughing Reverted. See talk. coming to the doorway with a blanket over her arm and dropping it on the couch The couch is yours. Goodnight, Vlad.

Vlad

lying on the extended coach Reverted? Okay, fine. Anyways, goodnight, Marliene.

She puts the kitchen light off and the flat goes quiet. He sleeps on his back with the blanket over him.
