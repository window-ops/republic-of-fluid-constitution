The suit§

The train to Prevessa is composed of eight cars behind an old electric unit, and the middle car has the arrangement where four seats face each other across a fixed table. Families with baskets have taken most of them, so Vlad has ended up standing at the last table with his hands on the edge, and a young woman stands opposite him in the same position, her canvas bag stowed on the rack above her head. Fields go past in long green bands, hedgerow, copse, hedgerow. She boarded at Leriduson with a paper book she has yet to open. For some minutes she has been looking at him not saying anything.

Vlad

leaning slightly closer to her across the table Hey, your red dark suit suits you.

Marliene

glancing down at her own sleeve, then back up Thank you. a small, careful smile I put it on this morning for reasons I have trouble explaining to myself.

Vlad

Why?

Marliene

I wore it the week I left Prevessa for Leriduson, and I have kept it since without wearing it once. smoothing the cuff flat with two fingers Now I am going back for a few days, so it seemed right to arrive as the person who left.

Vlad

When you left Prevessa and why?

Marliene

Four years ago, at twenty. shifting her weight as the car sways I wanted a town with more in it than the abattoir and the seed registry, and Leriduson was close enough that I could come home if I wanted to.

Marliene

Marliene

Marliene

Organic Law 44 of 1998 on Interpersonal Communication Services
Part 3, Section 12: Mandatory Interoperability Profile

12.1. Every provider of an interpersonal communication service to residents MUST implement the federated messaging profile defined in Annex II. A provider MUST NOT require a proprietary client as a condition of access.

12.2. Voice and video sessions MUST be negotiated over the profile in Annex III. A provider MAY offer circuit-switched carriage in addition, and MUST NOT offer it as the only means.

12.3. Identity MUST be portable. A resident who changes provider MUST retain the identifier, and the outgoing provider MUST serve redirection records for no less than five years.

12.4. A provider MUST NOT make delivery conditional on the recipient holding an account with the same provider.

12.5. Reference deployment. A conforming home server is obtained and built as follows. Deviations MUST be recorded in the register of the operating cooperative.

git clone https://vcs.telephony.rof/core/homeserver
cd homeserver
./configure --with-federation --with-webrtc --without-telemetry
make && make check

12.6. Message content MUST be treated as untrusted. Before rendering, a client MUST neutralise every construction listed in Annex VII, which includes: script elements; event handler attributes; javascript, data and vbscript URI schemes; style declarations carrying expressions or url() references; svg documents carrying script or foreignObject; iframe, object, embed and applet elements; meta refresh directives; base elements; form elements and their controls; and markup constructions which terminate an enclosing attribute or comment.

12.7. A client which cannot neutralise a construction MUST refuse to render the message and MUST display the raw source instead.

Annex VII goes on for nine pages, and my friend at the cooperative says it is the only law he has ever read that he could compile.

Vlad

And when I checked the nuclear reactor operations law, it was written by real nuclear engineers.

Marliene

That is how the drafting works here, the Assembly seats people by lot and by trade, so the ones who write a statute are the ones who have stood inside the building it governs. putting the phone face down on her knee My uncle sat on a committee about milk hygiene for a year, and he said the arguments were about scrub brushes and pipe diameters and nothing else.

holding the phone up between them

Organic Law 17 of 1986 on the Operation of Fission Plant
Title IV, Article 62: Terminology of Rapid Shutdown

62.1. The rapid insertion of all control assemblies for the purpose of terminating the chain reaction is designated in this Law by the term SCRAM.

62.2. The term is the abbreviation of Safety Control Rod Axe Man, being the operator stationed at the suspension rope of the control assembly of the first pile at Chicago in 1942, whose instruction was to sever that rope on command.

62.3. Alternative designations, including rapid shutdown, emergency shutdown, reactor trip and pile trip, MAY be used in operating documentation, in training material and in the annunciation of plant, provided that each is defined at first use as referring to the operation designated by 62.1.

62.4. The designation in 62.1 is the operative one in law. Where a document of the operator conflicts with this Law, this Law governs.

scrolling down a little further, turning the screen so he can see And there is a drawing at the foot of the article, a man with an axe standing over the rods, which somebody put in the printed edition and nobody has ever moved to remove.

Ordinary Law 209 of 2011 on Competitive Simulated Firearm Contests
Title II: Conduct of Play

Article 14: A contest under this Law is played between two teams of five on a symmetric map. One team is charged with the placement of an explosive device at a designated site, the other with its prevention and with the removal of the device once placed.

Article 15: A round is concluded by the elimination of one team, by the detonation of the device, or by the expiry of the round clock, whichever occurs first. The removal of a placed device concludes the round in favour of the preventing team notwithstanding any remaining time.

Article 16: Resources accumulated by a team persist between rounds. A team which has lost consecutive rounds receives an increased allocation, and the drafters record their opinion that a team spending that allocation in full on the following round is usually making a mistake.

Article 17: The projectile deviates from the axis of aim under sustained fire in a pattern determined by the weapon. A player is advised to learn one pattern thoroughly rather than four of them badly, and to fire in bursts of two or three at any range beyond the width of a corridor.
