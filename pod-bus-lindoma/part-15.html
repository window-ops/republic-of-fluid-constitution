The way home§

His lands in the evening. The next day he takes the ferry across to Lutoliluri and the connection up the coast to Cortius, and changes there onto the hill line for Corcina. Parking it forty kilometres off a Bloc coast was idiotic, they can put it off their water whenever they decide to, and if anything goes wrong out there it goes wrong in front of a Fluidian court with me as the person who countersigned every one of those agreements.

Linda

Vlad

Linda

I kept waiting for someone to come and interview me, and the cooperative told me there is no service that does that, so it has been sitting in my head with nowhere to go.

Linda

That makes me the one who leaked it. I stay inside the Bloc for the rest of my life, Fluid, Iosmein and Aporia, three countries and a ferry.

Linda

Vlad

Linda

Corcina, for now. The cooperative at Cortius found me a room up there and a woman who teaches Fluidian twice a week. After that, I have no idea.
