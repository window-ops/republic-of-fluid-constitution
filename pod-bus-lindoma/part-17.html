Out of Leriduson§

Marliene is up before him and the kettle is already on when he wakes up. They stand around in the kitchen for an hour, talking about the yard, about her partner's shifts in Spain, about nothing much. She carries his bag down through the six streets again and hands it back to him under the gate. On the platform she holds on to him for a few seconds, says adio, and goes off without waiting for the train to leave. The Torvena unit goes out on time, past the sheds and the cranes, and the line climbs out of the town onto the moor. His phone goes in his pocket somewhere before Bosquela.
