The place at the edge§

It is the middle of the afternoon, three days after the bulletin. He walks out of Marginal along the road toward the semi-center, and stops at the place where the estate ends and the older streets begin. It is half canteen and half cafe, tables along the window, a stand at the back with the pots and the coffee machine on it. He has not been inside since the year he moved to Marginal.
