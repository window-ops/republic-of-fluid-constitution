Calmena

Calmena is a town of 154,000 residents in the sub-region of Tessilius, in the Western Region of the Republic of Fluid. The textile town of the river terraces. The repair workshops closed under the Directorate were re-established here.

Setting

The town stands where the river drops through a series of terraces. The fall of water powered the first mechanised looms, sited here in the 1860s.

History

The looms pulled the first industrial workforce out of the villages, and the Unionist Party had its strongest branches here before 1890. The Directorate closed the repair shops in 1961 because repair produced no tonnage. They reopened in 1975 under the Assembly Reform, and the Republic's present duty of repairability in Article 33 is drafted from the Calmena workshops' own rules.

Work and provision

Cloth, clothing and footwear cooperatives, the national repair training school, and the tool library network that supplies the western region.

Government

Elected communal council under Article 141(2).
