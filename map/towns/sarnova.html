Town at the closed southern frontier

Sarnova

Sarnova is a town of 71,000 residents in the sub-region of Aspreduson, in the Western Region of the Republic of Fluid. The western frontier town, facing a border that has been shut since 1939.

Setting

The town stands on the southern territory, where the road once ran into Bor Brashavenko.

History

The mercenaries who retook Lindoma in 1848 crossed here, which the town has not forgotten. The kingdom of Bor Brashavenko aligned with the Axis in 1939 and closed the border, and it has not reopened in eighty-seven years. Whether that state is life-limiting within the meaning of Article 288 is a finding the Assembly must make by July 2028.

Work and provision

Civil protection, upland farming, and the southern monitoring station. The garrison function ended with the Assembly Reform, and the barracks now house the regional civil protection stores held under Article 284.

Government

Elected communal council under Article 141(2).
