Vestrano

Vestrano is a town of 79,000 residents in the sub-region of Esteveduson, in the Eastern Region of the Republic of Fluid. The town where the southern routes converge before Sornorum, holding the haulage depots, warehousing and rail sidings of the southern network.

Setting

The town stands on the flat approaches south-west of the Sornorum junction.

History

Built around the railway in the 1900s and rebuilt around the motorway in the 1960s. The vehicle dimensions, masses and loads that the Republic fixes identically throughout the territory under Article 178(1) are tested here, and the town therefore follows the standardisation question closely.

Work and provision

Haulage and logistics cooperatives, vehicle building and repair, the national standards testing ground, and the southern materials recovery works.

Government

Elected communal council under Article 141(2).
