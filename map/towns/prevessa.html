Prevessa

Prevessa is a town of 94,000 residents in the sub-region of Anselaqui, in the Eastern Region of the Republic of Fluid. The market town of the mixed farming country inland from Leriduson.

Setting

The country around the town is rolling, with small fields, hedgerow and copse. It is farmed in mixed rotation. The grain basin further south is farmed in monoculture.

History

Prevessa was never industrialised, requisitioned, struck or audited. It supplied food through two blockades and the Directorate never found a use for it.

Work and provision

Mixed farming, dairy, the regional abattoir operated to the husbandry standards of Article 31(5), and the eastern branch of the seed and breed registry.

Government

Elected communal council under Article 141(2). Fourteen of the surrounding communes are governed by assembly in person.
