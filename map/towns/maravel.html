Fishing port on the north-western bay

Maravel

Maravel is a town of 121,000 residents in the sub-region of Merrisoma, in the Western Region of the Republic of Fluid. The fishing town of the north-west, on a shallow bay of salt marsh, mudflat and dune.

Setting

The inner bay is shallow, with a large salt marsh behind it. The marsh is protected against conversion under Article 30(1) and is the principal wintering ground for migratory birds on this coast.

History

A herring town from the eighteenth century. The fleet was requisitioned for supply runs under both blockades, and thirty-one boats were lost between 1940 and 1945. Catch limits computed below documented regeneration were introduced here first, in 1979, over the objection of the fleet.

Work and provision

Fishing and processing cooperatives, boat repair, marsh restoration, and the marine research station that supplies the Ecological Budget Authority with its harvest figures for the northern populations under Article 25(1)(e).

Government

Elected communal council under Article 141(2).
