Durorilaqui

Durorilaqui is a town of 247,000 residents in the sub-region of Durorilaqui, in the Western Region of the Republic of Fluid. The principal agricultural town of the Republic, on the plain supplying most of its grain, and the town worst affected by the Hungry Springs.

Setting

The town stands at the centre of the western arable plain, on the deepest soils in the country.

History

Between 1891 and 1894 land was taken from the great estates and handed to village committees faster than the committees could be trained to run it, two harvests failed, and roughly nine thousand people died, most of them within eighty kilometres of this town. The Council slowed the transfers and replaced the commissioners with agronomists. The rule the Republic learned then was written down for the first time only in 2026, as Article 25(1)(d): the knowledge held by the people who live in a place is evidence.

Work and provision

Grain, roots and dairy cooperatives, the agronomy institute, and the national seed heritage store. Cultivated varieties and breeds developed by inhabitants are recorded and protected under Article 29(6), and their appropriation by exclusive right is void.

Government

Elected communal council under Article 141(2). The surrounding communes are almost all governed by assembly in person.
