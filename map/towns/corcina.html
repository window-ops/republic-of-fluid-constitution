Corcina

Corcina is a town of 87,000 residents in the sub-region of Belvacius, in the Eastern Region of the Republic of Fluid. A hill town of the south-east, among the wind installations and the upland pasture.

Setting

The town stands on high ground running down to the south-eastern coast. The ground is exposed and the district is thinly settled.

History

Quiet until the energy transition, when the hills turned out to be the best wind resource in the Republic. The first installations were sited in 1998 after a procedure that the commune describes as inadequate. Article 32(5) now requires the participation of the affected commune and compensation for burdens borne on behalf of everyone else.

Work and provision

Wind generation and maintenance, upland sheep, forestry, and the regional grid control room.

Government

Elected communal council under Article 141(2).
