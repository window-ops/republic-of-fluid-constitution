Chemical works town on the Marnaqui

Tavrino

Tavrino is a town of 118,000 residents in the sub-region of Marnaqui, in the Eastern Region of the Republic of Fluid. On the Marnaqui river, contaminated under the Directorate and now under restoration.

Setting

The town stands on the Marnaqui river, in the polluted area.

History

The chemical works were expanded under the Directorate and the river was dead below them by 1966. The Cortius Report of 1968 said so and was suppressed. Remediation began in 1977 and the first fish returned in 1994. Contaminated sites are now inventoried, published and remediated on a binding timetable under Article 34(4), and the Marnaqui inventory is the oldest entry on the national list.

Work and provision

Chemicals and pharmaceuticals produced within the release ceilings of Article 23(1)(g), remediation works, and the river monitoring station whose continuous data feed the published record required by Article 35(1).

Government

Elected communal council under Article 141(2).
