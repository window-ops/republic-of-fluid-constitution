Sederna

Sederna is a town of 168,000 residents in the sub-region of Lindoma, in the Western Region of the Republic of Fluid. Inland from the capital where the northern and western lines meet. A town of teachers, engineers and railway workers since the 1870s.

Setting

Sederna occupies the plateau south of Lindoma. The town shares the maritime climate of the capital sub-region, mild and wet with little range through the year.

History

The Scientific Party was founded here in 1881 among schoolteachers and railway engineers, and merged into the Humanist Socialist Party in the 1920s. The teacher training college dates from 1897 and it's attributed to the literacy rises, where literacy passed ninety per cent in 1912.

Work and provision

The rail workshops, the teacher training college, the national institute for measurement and calibration which maintains the standards published under Article 178, and the largest seed store outside Durorilaqui.

Government

Elected communal council under Article 141(2).
