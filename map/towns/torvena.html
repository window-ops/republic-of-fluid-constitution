Moor town, wool and stone

Torvena

Torvena is a town of 96,000 residents in the sub-region of Kelvaqui, in the Western Region of the Republic of Fluid. The market town of the northern plains, on the edge of the high moor. Wool, building stone and, until recently, coal.

Setting

The town stands at the foot of the moor, where the upland streams gather. Winters are long, the soil is thin, and the rainfall is the highest in the Western Region.

History

A drovers' town before the Republic. The pits opened in the 1860s on seams that were thin and poor even by the standards of the time, were largely worked out by the 1930s, and closed for good on the fossil schedule fixed under Article 32(2). Uranium was found under the moor in 1956 and has mattered a great deal more to the town than the coal ever did. Torvena has voted for the Highlands Federation at every election since the Federation was founded.

Work and provision

Uranium mining on the moor, wool and cloth cooperatives, the quarry, upland restoration work, and the sheep and hardy cattle of the moor edge. The transition from coal is settled; the question now is what Article 34(5) requires when a single sub-region has both the workings and the spoil. Its representatives argue that Article 181 equalisation has been applied as a formula, and they hold that it states an entitlement.

Government

Elected communal council under Article 141(2). Most of the communes around it are small enough to be governed by assembly in person under Article 141(1).
