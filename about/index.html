How this register is built

This register loads no font, no script and no image from outside the Republic. Article 109(5) requires public bodies to work on free and open software whose source is public, and Article 301 requires public information systems to run without the consent, licence, account or continued existence of any foreign supplier. A register fetching a typeface from another jurisdiction would breach both.

Every reference between articles is a link in both directions, and each article shows which articles cite it. The colours are taken from the state flag.

The summary, the concept pages, the glossary and the everyday-words table are guidance and have no legal force under Article 315(3). Where any of them differs from the text, the text governs, and Article 315(4) gives any person the right to report the divergence and receive a reasoned answer. This is the only page of the register that states it.
