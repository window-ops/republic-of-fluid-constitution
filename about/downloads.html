What may be done with them

The files have no licence condition, no registration and no account. Article 314(2) requires the consolidated text to be published in a form that can be read, copied and processed by machine, and Article 314(3) requires the table of cross-references to be published with it.

Anyone building a reader, a translation or an offline copy works from these. The register asks nothing in return and keeps no record of who takes them.
