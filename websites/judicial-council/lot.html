Drawn by lot

Thirteen residents, chosen at random from the whole population, trained before they take part, free to decline without giving a reason.

Article 210(1): the thirteen resident members are drawn by lot from the residents of the Republic, by a public and verifiable procedure, composed so as to reflect the population in age, gender, territory, occupation and condition.

Article 210(2): participation is voluntary. A person declining is replaced by a fresh draw, and no reason need be given.

The draw

The Electoral Commission conducts the draw under Article 244(3). Article 235(1) publishes the method and the code of the drawing procedure under Article 109(5), and the result is published upon the draw together with the composition of the pool it came from.

An independent body designated by organic law verifies annually that the draws of Article 201, Article 210 and Article 217 were conducted as published, and its report is public.

Declining

Participation is voluntary, and a refusal requires no reason. A person declining is replaced by a fresh draw. Provision for transport, care and accommodation under Article 210(5) exists so that the cost of serving falls on nobody.

Composition reflecting the population

The procedure composes the thirteen so as to reflect the population in age, gender, territory, occupation and condition. The method by which that is done is published with the code of the draw, and the composition attained is published with the result.

The Office of Democratic Integrity reports annually on the observance of the participation this Constitution requires, in the terms of Article 143(9)(b).

Training

The training lies with institutions independent of the courts and of this Council, and makes the materials public. A resident member takes part in no formation before completing it.
