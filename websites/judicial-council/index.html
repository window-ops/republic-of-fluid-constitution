Organ of the Republic

The Judicial Council

Twenty-one members administer the judiciary. Thirteen are residents drawn by lot, and they hold the majority in every formation of this Council. Eight are judges elected by the judges of the Republic.

13 residents drawn by lot, Article 210 8 judges elected by the judges, Article 211

Article 207(1): the Council administers the judiciary, and it is independent of the Assembly, of the executive committees, of the planning bodies and of the courts.

Article 207(2): the Council in no case decides a case, reviews a judgment, instructs a judge or exercises influence over the outcome of any proceeding.

Article 209(2): the members drawn by lot hold the majority in every formation of the Council, including its disciplinary panels and its appointment boards.

What this Council does

Function Provision
Conducts the competitions by which judges are recruited Article 208(1), through Article 202
Appoints judges Article 208(1)
Confirms the justices drawn to the Constitutional Court Article 208(1), through Article 217
Confirms every person drawn to an oversight office Article 237(1)
Constitutes the disciplinary panels Article 206(3)
Maintains the assignment system by which cases reach judges Article 201
Publishes the statistics of the courts Article 208(1)
Proposes to the Assembly the resources the judiciary requires Article 208(1)
Appoints the head of the prosecution service Article 208(2), through Article 224(2)

Everything happens in public

Sittings are public and transmitted, open to journalists by right. Article 213(2) records deliberations and publishes the record within seven days, with the votes by name and the reasons of every member who dissents. Article 213(4) forbids deliberation in secret and voids a decision taken in a sitting closed to the public.

Where this Council meets another organ

Organ What passes between us
Constitutional Court Confirms each justice drawn, and applies to that Court for the removal of a member of an oversight office under Article 239(1). It alone may remove a resident member of this Council.
Judicial Inspectorate Opens the disciplinary proceedings this Council hears. Article 214(3) forbids this Council to appoint, instruct, remunerate, discipline or remove it.
Prosecution service Its head is appointed by this Council by open competition, for a single term of seven years.
Electoral Commission Conducts the draws by which the resident members of this Council are chosen, under Article 244(3).
Audit Office Audits the records of this Council annually, and publishes the audit in full.
National Court Judges of every level stand for the eight elected seats, and Article 211(2) allows no level more than three of them.
