Publicity

Sittings are public and transmitted. The record appears within seven days, with the votes by name and the reasons of every member who dissents.

Article 213(1): sittings are public, are transmitted, and are open to journalists by right under Article 196(3).

Article 213(2): deliberations are recorded, and the record is published within seven days, together with the votes by name and the reasons of every member who dissents.

Article 213(4): the Council in no case deliberates in secret, and a decision taken in a sitting closed to the public is void.

Decisions are taken by a majority of the members present, by open vote recorded by name. The full record names every member and carries the reasons of each who dissented.

Files

Files of appointment, of promotion and of discipline are public, with the redactions Article 51 requires. Article 202(4) requires the results of a competition, the marks, the criteria applied and the composition of every examining board to be published.

Closing part of a sitting

Article 213(6): where the Council closes a part of a sitting for the protection of a complainant, it states the ground, publishes the order, and publishes the record upon the expiry of the period the order fixes.

This is the only ground on which any part of a sitting closes, and the record follows in every case. Article 213(4) voids a decision taken in a sitting closed to the public.
