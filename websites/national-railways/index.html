The railway branch, federated since 1993

Eight route groups, one timetable

Every cooperative that runs a train in the Republic is a member here. Eight route groups operate the main lines. Twenty sub-regional operators run the branches inside them. The federation holds the timetable, the stations, the gate record and the card. Podnik builds the vehicles and publishes where they are. We run them.

  • 8 route groups
  • 20 sub-regional members
  • 145 stations
  • 6,210 route km
  • 87% electrified

  • The card

    CARD-ROM is a removable device with no processor of its own. Minified NAND stores records, EEPROM saves the options and the display language, and the gates and machines that read it run FreeRTOS. It is issued without charge at any machine.

The division of work

The federation and Podnik

Podnik builds, we operate. The thirteen factories of the transport federation build the rail vehicles, hold their drawings and guarantee their parts. The cooperatives here bring the vehicles into traffic, staff them, maintain them in the depots the sub-regions provide, and answer for the timetable. A route group that wants a different vehicle sends a design proposal to the Podnik assembly. The southern cooperative did that for the R8/A in 2026.

The position feed comes from them. Every vehicle in traffic reports to api.podnik.rof. Our application reads that feed as any other client does, with no key and no privilege. The timetable, the station identifiers and the gate counts are ours, and we publish them on the same terms: no account, no quota, public domain under Article 10.

The rail stock is electric. The wire is at 25 kV, 50 Hz over 5,425 km. The remaining 785 km are operated by the SED R8 and its variants on the 220 kWh reserve battery. The Torvena branch reopened in 2017 on that reserve. The railway keeps thirty-four diesel yard locomotives, and they stay within the yards and sidings.
