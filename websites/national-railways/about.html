The federation

What it is, and what it may not do

The National Railways Federation is the branch federation of the railway operating cooperatives, constituted under Article 89 and seated at Lindoma Central. It has held the timetable, the stations and the technical knowledge of the branch since 1993. It employs no one to run a train; its members do that.

  • 1993 constituted
  • 8 route groups
  • 20 sub-regional operators
  • 38,600 workers, all members
  • 29 depots

Standing

What this federation is

  • A branch federation

    Constituted under Article 89. Membership is compulsory for every cooperative operating a train. The federation holds the technical knowledge of the branch, its reserve and its solidarity fund, distributes the branch allocation, and represents the branch in the planning procedure.

  • Governed by delegates

    The assembly is composed of delegates elected by the assemblies of the member cooperatives, mandated, recallable at any time, and serving limited terms. The federation directs the internal affairs of a member in the cases the Constitution and organic law provide, and in no other.

  • Answerable in public

    Each member is reviewed yearly against its ceiling, its plan, its ecological account and the needs it undertook to meet, under Article 101. The review and the answer of the member are published together. Where the fault lay with the plan, Article 103 requires the federation to say so.

  • Holding no public authority

    The federation exercises no public authority and represents no organ. Local and inter-communal transport belongs to the sub-region under Article 174. The federation runs what crosses between them.

Data

What the gate record is, and what it is not

The record is a count of journeys. A card presented at a gate leaves one line: the station, the hour, the section, and how many people the record covers. The route group adds those lines up to size its trains. Article 51(4) puts the totals in the knowledge commons and prohibits any trade in the data behind them.

No machine decides anything about a passenger. Article 51(5) reserves a decision with legal effect to an identified person. Article 51(7) prohibits scoring, ranking and profiling in the allocation of provision. There is no passenger score, no history held against anyone, and no consequence for travelling often or seldom.

The card holds its own history. The last ninety days of records sit in the NAND on the card. Whoever holds the card can read them at any machine. Older records are overwritten. The federation keeps the totals and deletes the card identifiers within seven days.

Since 1993

How it came to hold what it holds

The lines were federated before the vehicles were. The 1907 artery and the branches around it were operated by separate cooperatives through the trade years, each with its own timetable. In 1993 the operating cooperatives constituted this federation and put the timetable, the paths and the station identifiers in one place.

The card came later. The Dubrazec standard was signed in Reformed Luces Aporia and adopted here in 2004, replacing a paper counting sheet the guards filled in by hand. Gates went in at the intercity platforms only, and they have never been fitted anywhere else.

The wire moved outward. The moor crossing to Leriduson was wired in 1996 and the lagoon coast in 2004. The Torvena branch reopened in 2017 on the battery reserve of the R8, which left 700 km worked without catenary and no plan to string more.

Reaching the federation

Where to write, and to whom

  • A fault, a delay, a station

    These belong to the route group that operates the line, and reach it fastest through the application, the machine on the concourse, or the short code 118. The federation forwards what arrives here and adds a day to the answer.

  • The timetable and the paths

    The federation office at Lindoma Central answers on the timetable, the change sheets and the station identifiers, in person during working hours and in writing at any time.

  • The card and the standard

    Questions on CARD-ROM, the Dubrazec standard, a fork of it, or the interfaces go to the technical office, which publishes its answers with the specification.

  • Accounts and reviews

    Minutes, resource accounts, ecological accounts and the yearly reviews under Article 101 are published as they are adopted, together with the answer of the member reviewed.
