Interfaces, specifications and source

Everything the railway runs on

Article 10 puts technical knowledge in the knowledge commons and prohibits a patent over it. What follows is the whole of what the federation holds: the wire protocol of the card, the interfaces the application reads, the guide for a transit system that wants to fork the standard, and the flags that decide what a build contains.

Versions

What changes, and how it is announced

  • The standard

    Revision 3 since 2021. A revision is agreed between the three networks of the Bloc and published a year before any gate requires it. Revision 3 cards are read by every gate and will be until the last of them expires.

  • The interfaces

    Versioned in the path. /v1 has stood since 2019 and will stand for two years past the announcement of a successor. Fields are added and never removed inside a version.

  • The firmware

    Released with the signature beside it. A gate refuses an image whose signature does not check, and a worker at the station has to unlock a gate that has been reflashed.

  • The station and route identifiers

    An identifier is retired and never reused, and the retirement is in the change feed for a year before it takes effect.
