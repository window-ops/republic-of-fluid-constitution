Ceilings and floors

Most items state a maximum. Habitat held in protection states a minimum, and restoration owed states a minimum quantity for the period. A head expressed as a floor is breached by falling below it, and the register above marks each accordingly.

Aggregate throughput of energy and materials is capped at a level the ecological limits sustain, and directs efficiency gains to sufficiency. A gain in efficiency therefore reduces throughput and does no work toward a larger volume of output.

Apportionment

The Constitution gives the distribution of a budget among regions, sectors and enterprises to the planning bodies of Title IV. They allocate according to the satisfaction of essential needs in the order Title IV establishes, to the equality of provision between territories, and to the least throughput sufficient to the purpose.

The Authority determines the total. The National Planning Congress apportions it.

Transfer between enterprises

Article 26(5): aggregation, transfer or trading of allocations between enterprises is permitted only under the procedure of organic law, within a sector, without any consideration passing, and with no increase in the total.

An allocation moves between two enterprises of the same sector where the plan is better served by the movement. Nothing is paid for the movement, and the sum of the two allocations is unchanged. Article 23(2) likewise counts materials and energy embodied in imports against the same budget and voids any offset acquired outside the territory.
