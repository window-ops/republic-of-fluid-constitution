Petition for revision

Any person may petition upon new evidence. The answer comes within ninety days and a refusal is reviewable by the Ecological Chamber.

Article 25(4): any person may petition for revision upon new evidence. The Authority answers within ninety days, and its refusal is reviewable under Article 27.

Article 25(6): revision that raises a ceiling requires published evidence that the higher figure remains within documented regenerative capacity, together with the concurring opinion of the scientific institutions organic law designates.

Requirements on limits

A petition to lower a limit is decided on the evidence. A petition to raise one carries two further requirements: published evidence that the higher figure stays within documented regenerative capacity, and the concurring opinion of the designated scientific institutions.

Review of a refusal

The Ecological Chamber of the Constitutional Court has the jurisdiction, and Article 19(1) gives any person standing to bring the action in the name of a natural entity, with no showing of personal injury, residence, nationality or economic interest. Article 19(3) excludes court fees, security for costs and adverse costs orders.
