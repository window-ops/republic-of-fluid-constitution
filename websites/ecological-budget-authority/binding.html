Binding force

A budget of this Authority binds the plan itself. An act exceeding one is void to the extent of the excess and confers no right.

Article 26(1): the ecological budgets bind the economic plan, the organs of planning, the executive committees, the communes, the regions, the cooperatives and every licensed entity.

Article 26(2): a plan, programme, licence, permit, contract or administrative act that exceeds an applicable budget is void to the extent of the excess and confers no right.

Article 26(4): where an allocation is exhausted, production dependent upon it halts, and the essential provision guaranteed under Title IV holds priority over all other use.

What follows from nullity

The excess portion of an act produces no legal effect from the outset. A permit issued beyond a budget confers no right to the quantity beyond it, and a contract concluded on that footing is unenforceable to that extent. The rest of the act stands.

An executive committee may not alter an ecological budget or to fix a ceiling in any case, and Article 265(1) makes void, whoever adopts it, an act attaining a result the Constitution prohibits. Any person may plead the nullity before any court at any time.
