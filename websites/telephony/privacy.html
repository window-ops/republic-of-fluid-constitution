What is never kept

There are no logs. That is a statement about how the machines are built. Promising good conduct would be the weaker thing, and the yearly audit examines the absence itself.

PositionAudited yearly

The short version

Telephony keeps no record of who called whom, who connected from where, who joined which room or who said anything to anybody. This is not a policy that a future assembly could quietly reverse. The equipment has nowhere to write such a record, the software that would write it was never installed, and the audit each year examines the absence itself.

How each part of it is impossibleEight places a record might have lived

Mechanism, not policy

  • Call detail recordsDo not existA billing system would need them. Calls are free, so no billing system was ever built, and the exchange software has the record-writing paths compiled out.
  • MediaNever traverses a serverCalls and small meetings are carried directly between the two ends. The mixer used above four participants forwards without storing and holds nothing after the room closes.
  • Connection tablesIn memory, never on diskThe IRC and Matrix servers hold current connections in memory. The machines have no writable storage mounted for that purpose.
  • Address leasesNot written downLeases live in memory and are gone at renewal. There is no historic table mapping an address to a household at a past moment.
  • Name service queriesAnswered and forgottenThe resolvers log nothing, filter nothing and answer nothing falsely.
  • Traffic inspectionNo equipment existsThe federation holds no deep packet inspection equipment. Buying any is outside its licence and the purchase would appear in the audited accounts.
  • Matrix mediaThirty days, then goneThe one exception on this page. It holds files people chose to upload, and it records nothing about anyone's behaviour.
  • Interception capabilityWas never builtThere is no lawful interception interface in the core, because the core is Open5GS as published.

The tradeStated plainly

What it costs and what is still visible

What this costs

Fault-finding is harder. The desk on 101 cannot look up what your line did last time, so it asks you and it believes you. Engineers work with live measurements on the pair in front of them.

Moderation is weaker. Channel operators act on what is in front of them, because nothing older exists to consult.

Abuse is harder to trace. Somebody misusing the network cannot usually be identified afterwards.

What is unavoidably visible

Your immediate neighbours on a shared cable can see that your pair is active, as anyone with a meter always could. The person you call knows you called them. A room you join sees you in it.

Outside the Republic, nothing is promised. Traffic leaving through the gateway at Corvaluri enters networks with their own arrangements.

CheckingThe Audit Office

How the absence is verified

The Audit Office examines which storage is mounted where, what the exchange software was compiled with, what the machines actually write, and whether anything has been added since the last visit to make sure they are absent as much as possible.

The report is published whole, in physical units where units apply, alongside the federation's accounts. Any person may put a question about it and receives a reasoned answer within the period the law fixes.
