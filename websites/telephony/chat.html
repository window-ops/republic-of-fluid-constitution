IdentityAny number of identities

Identity on the services

Resident identity and further identities

The resident identity works on both services without registration. Beyond that, a resident may keep any number of further identities under names of their own choosing, without giving a reason.

The federation keeps no link between a resident's identities. No table exists in which such a link could be written, and the audit checks for the absence of one.

Moderation without logs

Channel operators are elected by the people in the channel and act on what is in front of them. There is no message history for the federation to consult, no reporting queue holding old traffic and no appeal based on earlier messages.

Moderation is therefore weaker. The Republic has decided against any rule whose enforcement requires records of all users.
