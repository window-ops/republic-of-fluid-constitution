Variants

Each variant closes a deficit and states what closing it costs elsewhere. The Secretariat prepares them and in no case chooses among them.

Article 90(4): the variants and the assumptions on which each rests are published.

Article 98(3): where the Congress fails to reconcile, it decides by vote of its delegates, publishes the dissenting positions, and states the reasons for the choice made among the variants.

Closes the steel deficit by import. Materials and energy embodied in imports count against the same budget, so the headroom falls by the same quantity.

No variant matches those conditions.

Assumptions published with each

Every variant above is published with the assumptions it rests upon: the rate at which released labour transfers between sectors, the availability consequences of a deferred overhaul, and the emissions factor applied to imported steel. An assumption is a figure the Secretariat has chosen, so each is stated separately from the computation and may be contested on its own.

Objections to an assumption are answered in writing before a round concludes, as Article 97(2) requires.

Variants withdrawn

Variants prepared and afterwards withdrawn in this cycle.
Variant Reason for withdrawal
Meeting the care deficit by extending shift lengths in residential care Withdrawn. Arduous tasks carry a duty of rotation and of reduced exposure, and the variant increased both.
Meeting the steel deficit by drawing on the reserve Withdrawn. The reserve is fixed by the Congress and the Secretariat prepares no variant that presupposes a decision reserved to another body.
Closing the water deficit by raising the abstraction ceiling Withdrawn. The ceiling belongs to the Ecological Budget Authority and Article 26(2) voids a plan exceeding it.

A withdrawn variant remains published with the reason, so that the record shows what was considered and why it was set aside.
