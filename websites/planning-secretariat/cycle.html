The cycle

Five years, six stages, no fewer than three rounds of negotiation, and every stage public.

  1. Registration of needs

    Declarations under Article 63, and the statements of the workplace, user and neighbourhood assemblies under Article 92 and Article 93. This Secretariat receives and aggregates them.

  2. Provisional balances

    This Secretariat computes the balances of materials, energy, labour and emissions and publishes the deficits, the surpluses and the variants. The round now open stands at this stage.

  3. Rounds of negotiation

    Each level reconciles the offers and needs before it and refers upward only what it has failed to reconcile. No fewer than three rounds are held, and a round concludes only after the objections received have been answered in writing and published.

  4. Reconciliation and adoption

    A level records agreement only where the enterprises, the users and the ecological delegate have each been heard and answered.

  5. Execution

    With the in-year adjustment, published with its reasons within fifteen days.

  6. Public account of the period

    Examined by the assemblies that furnished the inputs. The account returns to the bodies whose declarations began the cycle.

The lowest level that can decide

Article 97(3): a matter is decided at the lowest level at which the effects of the decision fall, and a higher level takes up a matter only upon referral or where the effects extend beyond one level.

Most lines never reach the Congress. A commune reconciling its own housing programme settles it, and this Secretariat records the settlement without passing it upward. The lines that arrive at the Congress are the residue of what the levels below could not agree.

Publicity

Article 97(4): every stage is public. A plan adopted upon inputs withheld from publication is void.

The consequence attaches to the plan itself. An input withheld at any stage invalidates the plan adopted upon it, and any person may bring an action under Article 143(9) before the Constitutional Court.
