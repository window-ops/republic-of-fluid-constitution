For cities outside the Republic

A fleet renewal here leaves good vehicles with decades left in them. They go to cities outside the Republic with the drawings, the list of moulds and tools and ten years of parts, and the federation adds nothing to the cost of making them.

The sale

  • The drawings

    Complete, to the last bracket. Nothing here is patented and nothing is withheld, so a buyer who wants to build the next batch themselves may do so and owes the federation nothing for it.

  • The parts

    Ten years guaranteed from delivery, at the price of making them. After that the drawings remain, and any workshop anywhere can make parts from them.

  • The list of moulds and tools

    What moulds, guides and holders the work needs, with the drawings for those too. Buyers taking a whole class can also order the moulds and tools with it.

  • Two weeks of fitters

    Two mechanics from the factory that built the vehicle spend two weeks at the buyer's depot at the start, and answer the telephone afterwards.

Read this first

No vehicle in this register has ever collected a fare. Travel is free at the point of use in the Republic and there are no fare gates, ticket machines or validators anywhere in it. Nothing sold here is wired, cut or braced for them.

Fitting them later is possible and it is work. A validator needs a supply, a mounting and a data path, and on the older trams it needs a hole in a structural pillar. The federation will supply the structural drawings showing where a pillar may be cut and where it may not. It does no fare equipment work itself.

Doorway flow changes when you fit one. The times spent at stops in the catalogue were measured with open doorways and free boarding. An operator fitting validators should plan on longer stops and say so in the timetable.

Procedure

  1. Write to the federation

    Name the lot, the operator, the network it is going to and the supply it will run on. Enquiries arrive at any communal office or through the federation directly.

  2. The account travels with the vehicle

    Every lot leaves with its build and running accounts, and the buyer publishes them. A vehicle with thirty years of life left in it is worth more here than the material in it, and the account is how that is shown.

  3. Payment in euro

    Through the External Currency Translation Cooperative, which holds the licences on each side of the border. The federation quotes the cost of the work and the materials, and adds nothing to it.

  4. Two conditions

    The buyer runs the vehicles in public service and does not resell them to a state the Republic has found life-limiting.
