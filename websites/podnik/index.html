Register of vehicles in service

Everything that runs here, written down

Sixty-eight models across seven modes, from the Ferrista T4 on the Lindoma cross-town lines to the two lagoon boats at Marendo. Positions refresh every ten seconds on an open feed, and every second for the Lindoma pods. The drawings are free to copy, because nothing here is patented.

What the federation does

Four services

  • The catalogue

    Every model in service, with its dimensions, mass, capacity, traction, service life and the factory that built it. Schematics and parts lists sit alongside each entry.

    Open the catalogue

  • Live positions

    One open feed gives the position of every vehicle running, keyed to OpenStreetMap. Any app can read it and work out waiting times without asking permission.

    Read the feed

  • Fleet advice

    Communes and sub-regions bring their lines, gradients, passenger counts and depot space. Podnik returns a costed fleet in physical units, with the ecological account attached.

    How advice works

  • Surplus abroad

    Vehicles the Republic no longer needs go to cities outside it, sold with the drawings, the list of moulds and tools and ten years of parts. Buyers deal with the federation directly.

Where the older stock came from

Fourteen imported models

The Czechoslovak trade brought eight of them. The tram fleet at Ostrava was the pattern the Republic bought to: CKD T3 cars from 1968, the KT8D5 articulated cars from 1989 and the T6A5 from 1994, together with ZiU-9 trolleybuses, Karosa buses and coaches, Vagonka carriages and one class of yard locomotive. The same T3 that Ostrava still runs today runs here.

Support now comes from inside the federation. The builders stopped supplying long ago. Ferrista holds the tram drawings under the transfer of 1993, Ilvara presses trolleybus body panels from the original moulds, Calmena makes the drives and Sederna makes the carriage wheel units. Every imported vehicle in traffic has been through a factory of the federation at least once, and the T3 has been through twice.

The rest arrived by two other routes. Three models came from the Vetruna factory in Iosmein under the Bloc exchange of the late eighties, and three were bought second hand before the Republic existed. One of those three lost its parts support in 2011 and is kept running by exchange between depots.

Members

The thirteen factories

Each factory is a cooperative in its own right and sends delegates to the federation assembly. The first part of every model code is the factory's short name. All thirteen build to one set of dimensions, masses and axle loads, signed off at the test station at Vestrano. Between them they also hold the support of every imported vehicle still running.

  • FER

    Ferrista Factory

    Ferrista · Vandorum

    Tram cars and light rail bodies. The oldest factory still running in the federation, running since the Ferrista tramways of 1911.

  • SED

    Sederna Carriage Factory

    Sederna · Lindoma

    Rail carriages, driving trailers and locomotives. Sederna has built for the railway since the line reached the town in 1874.

  • COR

    Cortius Electric

    Cortius · Cortius

    Metro cars, traction motors and control gear. Supplies drives to seven of the other factories.

  • LIN

    Lindoma Pod Factory

    Lindoma · Lindoma

    Automated pods and articulated pod buses for the separate Lindoma lanes. Founded 2017 and the youngest member of the federation.

  • TAV

    Tavrino Motor Factory

    Tavrino · Marnaqui

    City buses, single and articulated. The largest output in the federation by unit count.

  • SOL

    Solvena Coach Factory

    Solvena · Quintorum

    Intercity and sub-regional coaches, including the high-floor bodies used on the long grain-basin runs.

  • LUT

    Lutoliluri Yard

    Lutoliluri · Sornorum

    Sea ferries for the Sluland route, plus ship repair for the whole eastern coast.

  • MAR

    Maravel Marine

    Maravel · Merrisoma

    Bay and estuary ferries with shallow draught, built for the salt marsh channels.

  • MND

    Marendo Boatyard

    Marendo · Ravelluri

    Lagoon ferries and small craft. The smallest member, with 84 workers.

  • ILV

    Ilvara Body Factory

    Ilvara · Thurioma

    Bus and tram shells, middle joints and the low-floor panels used across five factories.

  • CAL

    Calmena Traction

    Calmena · Tessilius

    Drives, battery packs, pantographs and brake systems. Builds no complete vehicle of its own.

  • DUR

    Durorilaqui Factory

    Durorilaqui · Durorilaqui

    Rural coaches, trailers and the trailer sets that serve the inland farming plain.

  • TOR

    Torvena Factory

    Torvena · Kelvaqui

    Minibuses and winter equipment for the northern moor, where the coach network thins out.

Travel costs nothing here. There are no fare gates, ticket machines or validators anywhere in the Republic, and none of the vehicles in this register is fitted for them. Buyers abroad should read the note on fitting them later in the surplus pages before ordering.
