Catalogue

Every vehicle running in the Republic belongs to one of the models below. Each entry contains what a depot, a commune or a buyer abroad needs to decide: the dimensions it must fit, the mass the road or track must support, what it holds, what it draws, and how long parts are supported. Fifty-five models were designed here. The fourteen imported ones are maintained from inside the Republic, most of them rebuilt in the factories of the federation.
