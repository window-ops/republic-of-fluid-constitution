What is published, and when

Stage What is public
Transmission from an oversight office The fact of transmission, published by the office transmitting it under Article 242(1)
Investigation Nothing, until a decision is taken
Decision not to prosecute The decision and the reasons, notified to the injured person
Conclusion of a case concerning a public office holder The file, the decision and the reasons
Annually Aggregate reporting of investigations touching office holders
