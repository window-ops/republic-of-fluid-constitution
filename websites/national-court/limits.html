Limits

The Court secures the uniform interpretation of the law. It decides no question of conformity with the Constitution, and it reopens no finding of fact.

Article 200(4): the National Court in no case decides a question of the conformity of a norm with this Constitution, which belongs to the Constitutional Chamber under Article 195.

What goes elsewhere

Question Decided by Provision
Conformity of a norm with this Constitution Constitutional Chamber, on reference Article 200(4) and Article 195
Actions under Title II and the remedies of Article 27 Ecological Chamber Article 221(1)
Findings of fact The regional court, on appeal, which decides finally on the facts Article 198(2) and (3)
Discipline of a judge A disciplinary panel of the Judicial Council, with appeal to the Constitutional Court Article 206(3) and (4)
Whether to prosecute The prosecution service, on the principle of legality, with reasons Article 226(1)
Conflicts of competence between organs and territories Constitutional Court Article 216(2), through Article 189

What no body may examine

Article 214(6): the Judicial Inspectorate in no case examines the content of a decision, and an inspection touching the reasoning of a judgment is void.

Article 241(4): an oversight office may in no case require the reasoning of a judgment.

Article 207(2): the Judicial Council in no case decides a case, reviews a judgment, instructs a judge or exercises influence over the outcome of any proceeding.

Article 206(1): disciplinary liability arises in no case for the content of a decision, for its reversal on appeal, or for a legal opinion expressed.
