Court of the Republic

The National Court

One law throughout the territory. This Court hears cassation on a question of law, resolves divergences between regional courts, and its rulings on divergence bind every court until the law changes.

Article 200(1): the National Court secures the uniform interpretation of the law throughout the territory.

Article 200(4): the Court decides a question of the conformity of a norm with this Constitution in no case, which belongs to the Constitutional Chamber under Article 195.

The three grounds of cassation

A question of law

The regional court decided finally on the facts. This Court examines the law it applied.

A divergence between regional courts

Two courts have decided the same question differently. A ruling settles it and binds every court.

A manifest breach of procedure

A defect in the conduct of the proceeding, of a kind apparent on the record.

The courts of the Republic.
Court Function
Communal and sub-regional courts Try civil, criminal, labour, cooperative, administrative and ecological matters at first instance
Regional courts Hear appeals on the facts and on the law, and decide finally

Where this Court meets another organ

Organ What passes between us
Constitutional Court Receives every question of conformity by reference under Article 195. This Court decides none.
Judicial Council Conducts the competitions by which judges of this Court are recruited, and maintains the assignment system of Article 201.
Judicial Inspectorate Examines the operation of this Court and the working of its assignment system, and the content of a judgment in no case.
Prosecution service Appears in criminal cassation, and its decisions not to prosecute are reviewable by a court under Article 226(2).
Audit Office Audits the resources of this Court annually, and the reasoning of a judgment in no case.
Defender of Rights May intervene in any proceeding concerning a right of Title III under Article 252(3).
