The Electoral Commission

Every election, referendum, recall and draw by lot in the Republic passes through this office. The record of each operation is published, and any resident may observe any of them.

Article 244 charges the Commission with the elections and referendums of the Republic, of the regions, of the sub-regions and of the communes, with the recalls of Article 128, Article 170(5) and Article 141(3), with the electoral list, with the registration of lists and candidacies, with the allocation of campaign resources, and with the proclamation of results.

Article 234 places the Commission outside the reach of the Assembly, of the executive committees, of the planning bodies, of the courts and of the other offices. No organ may instruct it, direct an inquiry, require the withdrawal of a finding, or alter a report before publication.

What this office does

Conduct the ballotNational votes are cast on paper, counted by hand, in public, in the room where they were cast. Each station publishes its count before the papers leave it. Register lists and candidaciesRegistration is refused only for want of a condition written in law. The programme, the ideology and the opinions of a list are examined in no case. Draw by lotJudges, lay judges, deliberative bodies and the colleges of the oversight offices are drawn here, in public session, by a published method. Draw the boundariesThe Boundary Division is composed afresh by lot for each apportionment and holds hearings in the localities it affects. Supervise electronic votingCommunal, sub-regional, regional and participatory procedures may use electronic means on the eight conditions of Article 245(4). National procedures may not. Decide disputesAny resident may contest the list, the ballot, the count, the computation or the proclamation, free of charge, with an appeal to the Constitutional Court.

Observing an operation

Any resident has the right to observe every operation of an election. Lists, associations and journalists attend as of right, and no permission is issued because none is required. Counting rooms are open from the close of the poll until the station count is signed and posted.

Stations are placed within reach of every resident, including in places of detention, in hospitals and in care institutions. A resident who cannot reach one may say so to this office, and the obstacle is recorded in the annual participation report.
