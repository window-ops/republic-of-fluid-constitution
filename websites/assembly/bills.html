Bills

The register lists every bill before the Assembly, with the assessment published alongside it.

Article 134(1): every bill is accompanied by an assessment, published with it, stating its effect on the rights of Title III, its effect on the ecological budgets of Title II, the resources its execution requires in physical units, and the persons and territories it affects.

Article 134(2): a bill whose assessment is absent or manifestly incomplete proceeds to no vote.

No bill matches those conditions.

The halted bill

The bill on communal records stated its ecological effect and its resource requirement and left two heads unaddressed: the effect on Article 51 and the persons and territories affected. The committee found the assessment clearly incomplete on 9 June 2026, and no vote may be taken until it is completed.

The finding is a decision of the committee and is published with its reasons. The bill resumes upon a completed assessment.

Consultation of communes and regions

The communes and regions affected by a bill are consulted. The consultation is published with the bill, together with what each territory said. Where a territory objects, the committee answers in writing.

Preventive review

Article 137(3): referral to the Constitutional Chamber is obligatory for organic laws, for the rules of procedure of the Assembly, and for any text restricting a right of Title III.

Article 137(4): referral is otherwise made within fifteen days of the final vote and before promulgation, by one tenth of the members and by the other applicants that Article names.

Two texts in the register above stand referred. The organic law on the drawing of lots is referred as of obligation. The bill on the licensing of foreign entities was referred and produced a declaration of incompatibility, published on the register of the Court.
