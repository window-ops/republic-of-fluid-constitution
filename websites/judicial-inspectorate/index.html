Inspecting body of the Republic

The Judicial Inspectorate

This body examines how the courts work. It examines what any judge decided in no case.

Standing prohibition

Article 214(6): the Inspectorate examines the content of a decision in no case, and an inspection touching the reasoning of a judgment is void.

Article 214(1): the Inspectorate examines the conduct of judges, the operation of the courts, the working of the assignment system, and compliance with the declarations of Article 204(4).

Article 214(4): the Inspectorate opens proceedings under Article 206(2), and decides them in no case.

Article 214(5): its reports are public, and it reports annually to the Assembly, to the Council and to the public.

Making a complaint about a judge

Disciplinary proceedings open upon complaint by any person or on the motion of this body. A complaint may be lodged at any communal office, by letter, or through the public information system, free of charge.

The grounds of liability are stated: a breach of the duties of Article 204, a failure to decide within the periods fixed, a breach of Article 201, concealment of an interest, and conduct incompatible with the office. It states equally that liability arises in no case for the content of a decision, for its reversal on appeal, or for a legal opinion expressed.

Where this body meets another organ

Organ What passes between us
Constitutional Court Appoints the head of this body in plenary, for a single term of seven years, from candidates presented by open competition.
Judicial Council Hears the proceedings this body opens. Article 214(3) forbids it to appoint, instruct, remunerate, discipline or remove this body.
Audit Office Audits the operation of the assignment system annually under Article 215(1). Article 241(4) forbids it to require the reasoning of a judgment.
Defender of Rights Its data division audits the assignment system jointly with the Audit Office under Article 254(4).
Office of Democratic Integrity Receives, verifies and publishes the declarations of Article 204(4). This body verifies compliance with them within the judiciary.
National Court Its assignment system and reassignment practice are examined here, and the content of its judgments in no case.
