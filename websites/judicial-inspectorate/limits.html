Matters excluded

The Inspectorate in no case examines the content of a decision, and an inspection touching the reasoning of a judgment is void.

Article 214(6)

The Inspectorate in no case examines the content of a decision, and an inspection touching the reasoning of a judgment is void.

The same limit elsewhere

Article 206(1): disciplinary liability arises in no case for the content of a decision, for its reversal on appeal, or for a legal opinion expressed.

Article 207(2): the Judicial Council in no case decides a case, reviews a judgment, instructs a judge or exercises influence over the outcome of any proceeding.

Article 241(4): an oversight office may in no case require the reasoning of a judgment.

Article 201(4): the president of a court, the Judicial Council and any other person may in no case select the judge who is to hear a case.

Article 204(6): judges may teach, write, research and take part in public debate upon questions of law, and this in no case engages disciplinary liability.

Where a decision is challenged

Complaint Where it goes
The judgment was wrong on the facts or on the law Appeal to the regional court under Article 198(2), which decides finally on the facts.
The regional court was wrong on a question of law Cassation to the National Court under Article 200(2).
A norm applied conflicts with the Constitution Reference to the Constitutional Chamber under Article 195.
The judge took too long, concealed an interest, or breached Article 201 The Inspectorate, which opens proceedings under Article 206(2).
A right of Title III was violated The Defender of Rights under Article 252(1).

Reason for the limit

The independence of the judiciary is established, and a body able to examine why a judge decided as they did would have a means of influencing the next decision. The prohibition therefore attaches to the inspection itself: an inspection touching the reasoning of a judgment is void, and produces nothing.

The remedy for a wrong decision is an appeal. The remedy for a slow, partial or concealed one is the Inspectorate.
