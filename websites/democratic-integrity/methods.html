Methods

Every method used to produce an indicator is published, open to public criticism, and revised upon reasoned objection.

What is published with each indicator

Item Form
The definition of the quantity measured Plain text, with the provision it is measured against
The source of every input Named body, date of supply, and the requirement relied upon
The computation Published code and parameters, reproducible by any person
The data Open and machine-readable, with the redactions the law requires
Known limitations Stated by the Office with each publication
Revisions Dated, with the objection that produced each one

Objecting to a method

How to lodge an objection

State the indicator, the step of the method objected to, and the reason. An objection may be lodged at any communal office, by letter, or through the public information system, free of charge.

Every reasoned objection receives a written answer. Where the objection is accepted, the method is revised, the revision is dated, and the indicator is recomputed for the periods affected.

Objections and answers are published together at /methods/objections in the open data of the Office.
