The Office of Democratic Integrity

Article 259(1): the Office monitors the conditions of participatory democracy and of social ownership, verifies the declarations of interest this Constitution requires, and investigates the appropriation of public resources.

Article 259(2): the Office publishes its findings and refers matters to the Constitutional Court and to the prosecution service, and holds no other power.

Limits of competence

Article 263(2): the Office may in no case ban, dissolve, suspend or restrict an association, refuse or annul a candidacy, exclude a person from office, attach an electoral consequence to a finding, or impose any sanction.

Article 263(1): the Office may in no case classify a person, a party, a list or an association by ideology, opinion, doctrine or programme.

Article 263(4): the Office may in no case maintain a register of persons by opinion, conduct surveillance, or acquire information otherwise than under Article 241.

The prohibitions in full, and why they are written this way

Findings address acts and conditions

Article 261(3): findings are addressed to acts, to measurements and to conditions. The Office may in no case attribute an ideology, a doctrine, an intention or an affiliation to any person or association.

A finding of the Office therefore states what was done, what was measured, and which provision is engaged. It states nothing about what anyone believes or intended.
