Indicators

Eight matters are fixed for the Office to monitor and report on each year. The Constitution fixes the matters and leaves the method to be published and criticised.

Concentration of the means of communication

Article 54(4)

18.0% held by the largest organisation

Ceiling fixed by the Constitution. Measured as the audience share of the largest single organisation across all media of record.

Independence and resources of media organisations

Article 54(3) and (5)

94 of 100 organisations report resources fixed independently of content

Six organisations report an allocation varying with the content published. Each case is stated in the annual report.

Observance of the participation required

Article 143(9)(b)

81% of required procedures held within the period

Nineteen per cent were held late or not at all. The bodies concerned are named in the annual report, and each has answered.

Accessibility of registration

Article 63(1)

99.2% of residents registered needs without an obstacle recorded

Obstacles recorded for 147,000 residents, most often in places of care. The Defender of Rights holds the individual complaints.

Operation of the assemblies

Articles 92, 93, 141 and 172

88% of assemblies met at the required intervals

Workplace, user, neighbourhood and communal assemblies. The shortfall falls almost entirely on user assemblies in two sub-regions.

Equality of provision between territories

Article 91(3)

6.4% spread between the best and worst served sub-region

Measured across the essential provision of Title IV. The spread has narrowed from 9.1 per cent in 2024.

Resources of the courts and the offices

Articles 193(5) and 240(2)

No reduction, withholding, delay or condition recorded

Reduction during the term of an Assembly is forbidden. This indicator has recorded no departure since the office was constituted.

Acts within Article 265

Article 265

2 acts referred to the Constitutional Court

Both referrals are pending. A referral states the acts found, the evidence and the provision engaged, and is published with the answer of the organ concerned.

Source of the figures

The same powers of collection reach every figure above. The Office may require documents, records, data, access to premises and systems, and the testimony of any person holding a public or delegated function. Commercial confidentiality is in no case a ground of refusal. Article 263(4) forbids acquiring information by any other means.

Audience shares are obtained from the media organisations themselves. Participation figures come from the bodies required to hold the procedures. Registration figures come from the communes. Provision figures come from the planning bodies. Each source is named against each indicator in the published data.
