The Defender of Rights

The Defender receives complaints about the violation of any right of Title III by an organ of the Republic, a territory, a planning body, a public establishment, a cooperative, a federation or a licensed foreign entity. It investigates, publishes findings, proposes remedies, and brings proceedings before the courts where a violation is not remedied.

Powers of the Defender

Article 252(1): the Defender receives complaints from any person concerning the violation of a right of Title III by any organ of the Republic, any territory, any planning body, any public establishment, any cooperative, any federation, or any licensed foreign entity.

Article 252(2): the Defender assists complainants, investigates, publishes findings, proposes remedies, and brings proceedings before the courts in its own name or in support of a complainant.

Article 252(3): the Defender holds standing under Article 231(3) and may intervene in any proceeding concerning a right of Title III.

Three divisions

Complaints

Any right of Title III, against any body listed above. This is the main work of the office and the route most people use.

Data and automated systems

The data division keeps the public register of every machine used in administration, planning, allocation and provision, and may order a prohibited one to stop.

Places of detention

The detention division visits without announcement, speaks with detained people in private, and may in no case be refused entry.

Limits of competence

It gives no order to a commune, quashes no decision and awards no reparation. Where a body fails to remedy a violation the Defender has found, Article 253(4) takes the matter to the competent court, and the remedies of Article 43 are ordered by that court alone.

It also has no power over criminal matters. A file disclosing an offence goes to the prosecution service, which decides under Article 226 and publishes the decision with its reasons.
