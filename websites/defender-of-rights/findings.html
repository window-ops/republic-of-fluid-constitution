Findings of no violation

The Torvena complaint was not upheld, and it appears above with the reasons. Provision stopped for eleven days through a fault in a records system, the commune restored it and made reparation, and the file disclosed no conditioning of provision upon conduct. Complaints not upheld are published on the same terms as complaints upheld.
