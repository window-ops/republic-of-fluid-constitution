Reaching us

This page sets out the languages, formats and routes by which a complaint may reach this office, and what the office furnishes at its own cost.

Language

A complaint is taken in any language of record. Interpretation is arranged by this office and paid for by this office. Where a complaint arrives in a language of record, the answer goes back in that language, and the finding is published in it alongside the version in the language of the body complained about.

Without writing

A clerk at any communal office writes down what is said and reads it back before stamping the receipt. The record so made is a complaint and carries the same standing as a written one. Nothing in Article 253 requires a complaint to be written by the person making it.

Formats

Form Available
Plain text and machine-readable data Published with each finding on the day.

Sign language interpretation at any meeting Arranged by this office at its own cost.
Printed copy of the Constitution and its summary Free to every dwelling and to any person on request.

No account, no device

The public information system takes a complaint without registration and without an account. Article 301 requires public information systems to run without the consent, licence, account or continued existence of any foreign supplier, and this office holds to that.

No device is required at any stage. Most complaints reach this office through a communal office.

Where the office sits

Lindoma

Main office. Open without appointment on every working day. Interpretation available on the day for the languages of record, and within three days for any other.

Cortius

Eastern office. Same hours, same terms.

Every commune

Any communal office receives a complaint for this office and forwards it the same day. Most complaints reach this office by that route.

Places of detention

The detention division visits unannounced. Ask to speak with it privately at the next visit, or write, and the letter leaves unopened.

Addressing the office about anything else

Article 242(6): every person may address an office, and receives a reasoned answer within the period fixed by organic law.

This covers questions, criticism of the conduct of this office, and objections to its methods. Every answer states reasons and criticism is published with the answer where the person writing agrees to publication.
