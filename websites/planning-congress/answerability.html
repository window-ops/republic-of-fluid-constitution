Answerability

The Congress answers to the bodies that compose it, and to the Assembly.

Article 96(3): the Congress answers to the regional economic councils, branch federations and user assemblies that constitute it, and to the Assembly under Article 143.

To the sending bodies

Delegates are mandated and recallable. A regional economic council disagreeing with the vote its delegate cast may recall that delegate, and four delegates were recalled in the current term. The mandate and the vote are both published, so the comparison is available to anyone.

The cycle closes with a public account of the period, examined by the assemblies that furnished its inputs. The bodies whose declarations began the plan examine what the plan did.

To the Assembly

Article 143 governs, and it gives standing to bring an action against the plan and against the planning bodies before the Constitutional Court. The Assembly is kept from adopting the plan or allocating resources itself, so the relation is one of scrutiny and of legal challenge. The Assembly gives the Congress no instruction.

A committee of the Assembly may require the attendance of any officer of a planning body and the production of documents. Delegates of the Congress appeared before Assembly committees on nine occasions in the year to June 2026.

To the oversight organs

Body What it does in respect of the Congress
Audit Office Transmits every departure from the plan or from a ceiling to the Congress, which answers within sixty days under Article 242(4)
Commissioner for Future Generations Heard by right at every stage of the cycle, and may require an act to be reconsidered in public sitting
Office of Democratic Integrity Monitors the observance of the participation this Constitution requires and the equality of provision between territories
Ecological Budget Authority Determines the totals the Congress apportions, and voids any apportionment exceeding one
Constitutional Court Hears actions against the plan and conflicts of competence under Article 189
