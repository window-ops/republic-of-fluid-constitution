Apportionment

The Ecological Budget Authority fixes each total. The Congress divides it among the regions and the branches.

Article 26(3): distribution of the budget among regions, sectors and enterprises belongs to the planning bodies of Title IV, which allocate according to the satisfaction of essential needs in the order established there, to the equality of provision between territories, and to the least throughput sufficient to the purpose.

The three criteria

Criterion What it requires
Satisfaction of essential needs in the order Title IV establishes An allocation is made first to what Article 62 guarantees, and afterwards in the order Article 91 fixes.
Equality of provision between territories Article 91(3). The Office of Democratic Integrity measures the spread between the best and worst served sub-region, presently 6.4 per cent.
The least throughput sufficient to the purpose Where two allocations meet the same need, the smaller is made.

Transfer between enterprises

Article 26(5): aggregation, transfer or trading of allocations between enterprises is permitted only under the procedure of organic law, within a sector, without any consideration passing, and with no increase in the total.

An allocation may move between two enterprises of one sector where the plan is better served. Nothing passes in exchange, and the sum is unchanged. Fourteen such movements were recorded in the current period, each published with the reason.

Exhaustion of an allocation

Production dependent upon an exhausted allocation halts, and the essential provision of Title IV takes priority over all other use. The same result follows at enterprise level, and the Audit Office publishes every departure from a ceiling.

Two heads presently stand above their limits: materials throughput and land conversion. The apportionment for the following period has been reduced accordingly on both.
