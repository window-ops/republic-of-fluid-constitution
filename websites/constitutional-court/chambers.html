The two chambers

Neither chamber is a standing body. Each is composed afresh by lot from among the fifteen justices for every matter it hears.

Article 220(2): the Constitutional Chamber is composed for each matter by lot from among the justices, in panels of seven, and is in no case a standing formation.

Article 221(2): the Ecological Chamber is composed for each matter by lot from among the justices, in panels of five, and sits with scientific assessors designated from the institutions named by organic law, who advise and hold no vote.

The Constitutional Chamber

The Chamber holds the preventive review of Article 137 and the references of Article 195. A court seised of a case in which a question of conformity arises refers the question here, decides its case upon the answer, and the answer stands published.

The Chamber may remit a matter to the plenary, and the plenary must then decide where the question concerns the distribution of functions under Article 13 or the amendment procedure of Title XII. Two matters were remitted in the period, both on the distribution of functions.

The Ecological Chamber

The Chamber holds the jurisdiction of Article 27 and the actions of Article 19. Article 19(1) gives standing to any person, in the name of a natural entity, with no showing of personal injury, residence, nationality or economic interest. Communes, cooperatives, unions, scientific institutions and associations hold the same standing.

Article 27(2): the Chamber may annul the offending act, enjoin the activity in the interim, order restoration on a fixed timetable, order the sequestration of resources sufficient to accomplish it, and declare the personal liability of those responsible.

Article 27(3): interim relief is granted where a credible risk of irreversible harm is shown, and the applicant furnishes no security.

Article 27(4): scientific uncertainty concerning the extent of a harm justifies no refusal of relief.

Article 221(3): the Chamber may require any measurement, sample, model or access from any organ, enterprise or licensed entity.

The scientific assessors

Assessors advise the Ecological Chamber and hold no vote. They are designated from the institutions organic law names, and the designation for each matter is published with the composition of the panel. Article 199(2) applies the same arrangement to the specialised divisions of the ordinary courts.

An assessor answers questions of measurement and of method. The finding on the law and the disposition of the case belong to the five justices.

Why the panels are drawn

Every case in the Republic is assigned to judges by lot, upon the order of receipt, by a procedure operating without human intervention. Article 220(2) and Article 221(2) apply the same rule inside this Court. Article 201(4) forbids the president of a court, the Judicial Council or any other person to select the judge who is to hear a case or to alter the composition of a panel after allocation.

The register of automated systems kept by the Defender of Rights holds the entry for the assignment system, and Article 254(4) has its data division audit that system jointly with the Audit Office.
