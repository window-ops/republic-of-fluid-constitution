Distribution

A printed copy to every dwelling in the Republic, and to any person who asks, free of charge.

Article 316(1): a printed copy of this Constitution, with the official summary, is furnished free of charge to every dwelling in the Republic, and to every person upon request.

Article 316(2): it is general provision under Article 107, is durable, and is produced within the ecological budgets under Article 33.

Article 316(3): copies are held and lent by every library, every school, every place of detention, every place of care and every communal office.

Editions

Every language of record

The text and the summary, published together, on the same date, at no charge.

Large print

Held by every library and communal office, and posted on request within seven days.

Braille

Produced to order and held at the regional libraries.

Audio

Read in full, published as open data, and playable without any account or application.

Easy-read

The official summary in a form tested with the deliberative body drawn by lot.

Open data

The text, the summary and the cross-reference table, machine-readable, at no charge.

Editions are produced in the formats accessibility demands.

Durability and the ecological budget

The printed copy must be durable and must be produced within the ecological budgets. A copy furnished to every dwelling is a substantial call on materials, so the edition is bound to last decades and is reprinted only upon amendment. Article 33 governs the repair and the useful life of goods generally.

The printing for the current edition consumed 1,840 tonnes of paper against an allocation of 1,900, and the figures are published in the account of this office.

Requesting a copy

Ask at any communal office, any library, or through the public information system. No reason is required, no account is created, and no charge is made in any format. Article 314(4) admits a charge in no case, for any version, edition, translation or format.
