Why this register is public

The licence attaches a disclosure duty stricter than the one borne by the cooperatives of the Republic. It reaches the volumes, the counterparties, the destinations and the declared end use of everything the company handles, and it reaches the ownership of the company itself.

The Audit Office may require the underlying documents at any time and does so at intervals it fixes. The Office of Democratic Integrity holds the ownership declarations. Neither needs the company's consent for either.
