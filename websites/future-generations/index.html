Oversight office of the Republic

The Commissioner for Future Generations

This office represents the interests of persons not yet born in the deliberations of the Republic. It is heard by right before the Assembly, the planning bodies and the councils of the territories, and it holds standing before the Ecological Chamber.

The register of what falls due

Obligations the Republic has already incurred, arranged by the year they come due. Choose how far to look.

2027

Retirement of the Vandorum coal boilers

The last combustion plant on the phase-out schedule. The schedule may be shortened and may not be lengthened.

2031

Restoration owed for the 2026 to 2031 period

A minimum quantity of restoration is fixed in each period. The quantity owed for this one stands at 41,200 hectares of soil and 610 km of watercourse.

2038

Certified lapse of the Sornorum reactor authorisation

Nuclear fission is transitional, and an authorisation lapses on the date certified. The Commissioner examines the retirement provision at intervals of no more than five years.

2056

Fifth review of the custody stores

Custody of nuclear waste, retirement of installations, restoration owed and maintenance of works come back to this office every five years, without end.

2076

Cumulative emissions budget exhausted on the present path

The greenhouse gas budget is expressed as a total remaining quantity. On present consumption the total is reached in fifty years, which is inside the lifetime of people already born.

2126

Sealed land of the 2026 spatial plans still sealed

Conversion, sealing and degradation of land are counted. Sealing is measured in centuries because that is how long it takes soil to come back.

2226

Second custody transfer of the Sornorum stores

The stores are engineered for two hundred years and require a successor arrangement before that. No institution of the Republic has existed that long, which is a fact this office states in every report.

12026

Radiological hazard of the spent fuel falls to background

Ten thousand years. Depletion transferred to the future is accounted a present cost, and the interval is recorded here for that purpose.

Long-term impact statements

A statement is attached to every bill, every draft plan, every spatial plan, and every proposal for an installation whose effects extend beyond thirty years. The statement computes the effect upon succeeding generations over the periods organic law fixes and is published with the draft.

One chapter of the current draft plan arrived without a statement, and one proceeds against an unanswered adverse opinion. Article 258(2) records both in the register, which is what this page does.
