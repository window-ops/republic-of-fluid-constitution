Rules

Everything on this page describes what most people already do. Where usage is divided the division is stated. The page carries the whole of the grammar; the words that refuse it are in the register, and what no rule predicts at all is on the quirks page.

The letters and their sounds

Fluidian is read as it is written and the reading is close to Romanian. One letter carries one sound, with the exceptions set out below.

The five vowels are /a e i o u/ and each keeps its value unstressed. Written i and u before another vowel are a full syllable of their own, so statiun is three syllables, /sta.ˈti.un/. The one exception is at the start of a word, where they glide: ia is /ja/.

Stress

Stress falls on the second syllable from the end. There are no exceptions in native words, and a loan that resists the rule is written with an acute on the stressed vowel until it settles. materinu /ma.te.ˈri.nu/, panelcu /pa.ˈnel.ku/, xuclar /ˈʃu.klar/.

Nouns and articles

There is no gender. The definite article is se and never changes, before a noun of any number, and it stands again before a direct object: me no jizde, I do not ride, against me no se jizde, I am not taking the ride. The indefinite article is un.

The plural adds -s after a vowel and -es after a consonant: persone, persones; statiun, statiunes. Numerals leave the noun alone where the number is already given: tri anu as readily as tri anos, and the survey of 2023 found the shorter form ahead in speech and the longer ahead in print.

Possession is carried by de and by nothing else: se colines de Belvacius, the hills of Belvacius; se ramu de porta, the door frame. There is no case ending anywhere in the language.

On the eastern seaboard a noun ending in a vowel takes -es as well, so cosa gives coses and porta gives portes. Both are understood everywhere and neither is corrected.

Pronouns

One set serves for subject and object alike, and the pronoun is never dropped.

The pronouns
Form Sense Note
me I, me subject and object alike: me no jizde, skuza me
ia I, emphatic for stress and in set phrases: ia dan zasta
tu you, singular -
vos you, plural also the form used to a stranger
el he, she, it no gender is marked anywhere
nos we, us -

ipse himself, herself, itself emphatic, never reflexive
sese oneself the reflexive

The possessive determiners are mie, vostre, nostre and sue, and they stand before the noun: se dum sue is also current, with the determiner after the noun, and both are printed.

Et points at a thing present, esto at a thing already named: et tren, this train; en esto loc, at the place just stated.

Adjectives

The adjective follows the noun and does not change for number: vent fort, strong wind; placa vetu, old signs. An adjective made from a noun normally takes -ak. A hundred and seven nouns refuse it and take an inherited form instead, and those are set out in the register.

Comparison uses plus with quam, and mas for the highest degree: plus grande quam, larger than; mas comun, commonest. The lesser degree has no word of its own and is said by turning the sentence round.

Two adjectives break the order. Prexa stands before its noun, giving prexa statiun, and the eastern prexim keeps the rule, giving statiun prexim. Nove is the adjective after a noun and the numeral nine in a designation, so un vuz nove is a new tram car and autovuz nove is bus nine.

Adverbs

The adverb is made from the adjective with -ment. An adjective ending in a vowel or in l, n, r or s takes the suffix whole, giving simplement and finalment. Everything else takes a linking -a-, giving certament and rapidament. The adverbs of time and degree are separate words and take no ending: nunc, ja, ancora, sempre, jamas, anque, circa, solmente.

The verb

The verb has one form. Person and number are carried by the pronoun and nothing else.

Tense
Tense Formed Example English
Present bare stem me jizde I ride
Past es and the participle in -t se tectu es cadut the canopy fell
Future va and the stem se tren va ariva the train will arrive
Negative no before the verb me no comprende I do not understand

An object pronoun stands before the verb it belongs to: me no vole vos desturba, I do not want to trouble you.

The bare stem is also the instruction, with no ending and no pronoun: abaxa e tena, get down and hold on; prende tot vos coses, take all your things. Broadcast wording uses it throughout.

A second form in -r stands after another verb and after a preposition: vole restar, wants to stay; sin tocar se placa, without touching the slab. It is listed as a separate entry in the lexicon wherever it is in use, because the set of verbs that have one is closed.

The participle is regular in -t on the bare stem: lesa gives lesat, cade gives cadut, prende gives prendet. One common verb divides by sub-region: the participle of fa is fat in most of the country and facit on the eastern seaboard.

One verb carries a set of forms that no other verb has. Jizde gives jizdu, jizder, jizdons, jizdez, jizdont, jizdent and jizdaie, all of them informal and all of them current. They are set out on the quirks page.

Numerals

The units are zero, un, du, tri, quatre, cinc, ses, sete, oct, nove, dece. The teens are compounded with a hyphen, dece-un, dece-du, and the same joint serves above the tens, vinte-quatre. The tens are vinte, trinta, quaranta, cinquanta, sesanta, setanta, octanta, novanta, and above them stand cent, mil, miliun and miliard.

A counted noun may stay singular, because the number has already been given: tri anu and tri anos are both current, with the shorter form ahead in speech and the longer ahead in print.

The hour is said with hora and the minutes bare: hora quatre e dece-du minut, 04:12. A decimal is read with punt, and a thousands mark is a full stop in print.

Suffixes

The load a suffix carries depends on what the word was before it. This is the part newcomers get wrong, and the register sets the exceptions out. Two endings compete for the abstract noun: -tate is the ordinary one, and an adjective ending in -al takes -ite, so actual gives actualite and total gives totalite.

Densification

One word in Fluidian often carries several senses that another language would separate. The academy named this densificatiun in 1968 and prefers the term to polymorphism.

Examples
Word Senses carried What decides
abaxa get down; lower a thing; slow a vehicle the object, or its absence
tena hold; hold a post; keep going a while longer whether a thing is named
jizde a ride; riding; to ride the article: se jizde is the ride, bare jizde is the act
trembuja construction; a highway; works in progress the sentence around it
nove new; nine position: after the noun it is the adjective, in a designation it is the numeral
praca work as a thing; work as an act the article

Fluidian densifies less than several of the languages around it and compresses almost nothing. A word is given more to do; the sentence keeps every word in its place, and the subject is not dropped. This affects emergency broadcast, where a sentence has to stay clear through a poor loudspeaker.

Word order

The order is subject, verb, object, and it does not move for a question or for emphasis. A question is marked by the question word and by nothing else: que es esto, what is this; quante dura, how long does it take. A yes-no question is the statement said as a question, and the answer is si or no.

The subject stands even where another language would drop it. An emergency broadcast keeps it too, which is why the wording runs longer here than the equivalent abroad.

The academy

Academia de Lingua Fluidian, constituted 1946 at Sederna. A cooperative under Title III, licensed by the Congress of Planning, holding no public function and issuing no binding rule.

Standing

The Republic has no official language. Article 16(1) gives every person the right to address public authority in a language they understand. Nothing on this site is law.
