Dictionary

The published lexicon. Search by Fluidian headword or by English sense. Pronunciation is given on every entry and follows the reading set out in the rules.

How the lexicon is kept

An entry is added when the academy has found the word in use in two sub-regions, or in one sub-region and in print. A sense is added to an existing entry on the same footing. Nothing is removed for being coarse; a word is removed only when the survey can no longer find anybody who uses it, and the removals are listed in the decisions.

The file is published in the open and may be taken and used by anybody, for any purpose, with no condition attached: lexicon.js. The academy asks only that a copy which has been altered says so.

The academy

Academia de Lingua Fluidian, constituted 1946 at Sederna. A cooperative under Title III, licensed by the Congress of Planning, holding no public function and issuing no binding rule.

Standing

The Republic has no official language. Article 16(1) gives every person the right to address public authority in a language they understand. Nothing on this site is law.
