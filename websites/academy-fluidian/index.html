The academy of the Fluidian language

Fluidian is the language most people in the Republic use. It holds no official standing. This academy describes it, publishes what it finds, and puts its findings to its users before adopting them.

What the academy is for

A language with no official status has no register of record and no ministry to keep one. It has a large number of speakers who need to be understood quickly, in a clinic, on a platform, in a queue, and in the seconds before an earthquake. The academy exists to establish what those speakers already say, to record it accurately, and to publish the record without charge.

Where usage is divided, the academy gives both forms. Where usage is settled, it records the settled form and marks it recommended. It does not correct a person who says the other thing, and no cooperative, commune or court is bound by a word printed here.

The academy

Academia de Lingua Fluidian, constituted 1946 at Sederna. A cooperative under Title III, licensed by the Congress of Planning, holding no public function and issuing no binding rule.

Standing

The Republic has no official language. Article 16(1) gives every person the right to address public authority in a language they understand. Nothing on this site is law.
