Phrasebook

What to say

Wording for situations in which speed of comprehension is the first requirement.

The emergency lines were shortened after the survey of January. A listener hearing a warning for the first time understood abaxa e tena in about a second and a half; the longer instruction it replaced took four. Broadcasters are under no obligation to use it.

What to say to nobody

These are current, everybody knows them, and each one is an accusation. They are printed because a person who does not know what they mean will meet them within a week.

Laku noc maimune.

Good night, monkey. Said of a politician, never to a person you mean no harm to.

Sali stronz.

A greeting in form and an insult in fact.

Ia trenu dan curtz.

I can only give you some shit. Directorate wall writing; coarse and still current.

Ia materinu.

I produced rotten work. It carries incompetence, and in most mouths corruption with it.

Se trembuja materinu.

The works are rotten. A formal accusation once printed; the reply is calitatev negativ.

Xuclar.

Corrupt. Naming a person xuclar in a meeting is a matter for the audit office, not for the room.

The academy takes no view on whether these should be said. It records that they are said, what they carry, and to whom they are addressed. Materinu and xuclar in particular are formal accusations when printed, and a cooperative named in either has to answer for the whole job.

The academy

Academia de Lingua Fluidian, constituted 1946 at Sederna. A cooperative under Title III, licensed by the Congress of Planning, holding no public function and issuing no binding rule.

Standing

The Republic has no official language. Article 16(1) gives every person the right to address public authority in a language they understand. Nothing on this site is law.
