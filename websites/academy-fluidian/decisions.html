Register of decisions

Decisions

Every decision is advisory. Each rests on a survey of users, the figures are printed with it, and a decision that later usage contradicts is withdrawn. The surveys are run twice a year on a sample of about 4,000 people, stratified by sub-region and by age.

214 · The numeral and the adjective nove

In force. Adopted March this year, on the third attempt at the question. Nove stays one word for both senses. Position decides: after a noun it is the adjective, in a designation slot it is the numeral. The proposal to write the numeral nov is dropped and will not be put again.

keep nove71%
write nov18%
no view11%

Sample 4,120. The proposal was strongest among people who set timetables and weakest everywhere else.

211 · Emergency wording

In force. The warning instruction is recommended as abaxa e tena. The longer form is not wrong and is no longer recommended. Measured on time to first correct action, not on preference.

abaxa e tena84%
longer form9%
neither understood7%

206 · The plural of a counted noun

Divided. Both tri anu and tri anos are recorded as current. The academy gives no recommendation and prints both in its own text according to register: the short form in speech, the long form in print.

tri anu, in speech58%
tri anos, in print61%
uses both39%

Respondents could choose more than one, so the figures do not add to a hundred.

199 · The prefix xu- outside xuclar

In force. Xu- is productive and may be attached freely. The academy records that in practice it attaches almost only to words about honesty, and that a speaker who attaches it elsewhere will be heard as making a joke.

productive63%
fixed in xuclar only29%
no view8%

187 · Loans from Japanese

In force. Four terms entered through the earthquake warning system and are recorded as loans in the technical register: the intensity step, the warning itself, and two instrument names. They are written as heard and stressed on the second syllable from the end like everything else.

158 · The plural of statiun

Withdrawn in 2019, having been in force since 1988. The academy had recommended statiunes in every register. The survey of 2018 found statiuny ahead in the eastern sub-regions and gaining, and the academy withdrew the recommendation.

statiunes47%
statiuny44%
uses both9%

A recommendation is withdrawn when the leading form falls below a two to one margin. The test was adopted in 1996.

143 · The formal register in broadcast

Withdrawn in 2003. The 1979 recommendation of the formal member of each alternate pair was contradicted by comprehension testing across every pair. See the register.

121 · Huclavy

In force since 1971. The name derives from huclav, hungry, by the ordinary place-forming suffix. The derivation from the Ostrava surname Hulvaky is recorded as a competing account and printed alongside. No survey was run; this is a question of evidence and the members were asked to weigh it.

How a decision is taken

  1. A question is put by any member, or by any person at all through the contact page.
  2. The philologists of the academy write a note setting out the forms in use and where each is found. The note makes no recommendation.
  3. A survey is run unless the question is one of evidence.
  4. The members vote. The result is published with the figures, the note, and any dissent by name.
  5. The decision is reviewed against the next survey that touches it, and withdrawn if the margin has gone.

The academy

Academia de Lingua Fluidian, constituted 1946 at Sederna. A cooperative under Title III, licensed by the Congress of Planning, holding no public function and issuing no binding rule.

Standing

The Republic has no official language. Article 16(1) gives every person the right to address public authority in a language they understand. Nothing on this site is law.
