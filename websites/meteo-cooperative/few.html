REP-FEW, the flood system

REP-FEW probes the rivers, the drainage and the coast of the Republic and warns where water is likely to arrive where it should not. It handles four kinds of flood, each with a different lead time and a different method. It was specified after the Sornorum floods of 2017 and entered service in April 2025.

In serviceSince April 2025
Kinds of flood handledFlash, riverine, coastal surge, and lagoon overtopping
Gauges312 river stage, 41 soil moisture, 9 tide, and 4 weather radars
Warning granularityReach for riverine, town or catchment for flash, coastal cell for surge
Administrative unitThe watershed, under Article 28 (2), which takes precedence over the sub-regional map
Specified afterThe Sornorum floods of July 2017

The four kinds of flood

Each kind arises differently and admits of a different amount of notice.

  • Flash flood: A convective storm delivers more rain than the drainage or the small watercourse can accept. This is the southern and eastern pattern, the one that produced the 2017 event, and it is becoming more frequent as rainfall concentrates into fewer heavy days. Notice amounts to between thirty and ninety minutes and depends entirely on the radar nowcast.
  • Riverine flood: Prolonged rainfall over a catchment raises a river until it leaves its channel. The Marnaqui, the longest river in the Republic, and the western watercourses draining the Vandorum upland behave this way. The catchment converts rainfall to river stage over hours or days, so notice runs from six hours on the small catchments to forty-eight on the Marnaqui.
  • Coastal surge: Wind and low pressure raise sea level above the astronomical tide. The north-western bay at Merrisoma is the most exposed, its shape amplifying a surge from the north-west, and the eastern shore is increasingly exposed as the dune barrier retreats. Notice runs from twelve to thirty-six hours, since the weather producing a surge is itself forecast that far ahead.
  • Lagoon overtopping: The Ravelluri lagoon lies behind a dune barrier that has retreated roughly nine hundred metres since 2013. Surge combined with a high tide overtops the barrier and floods the lagoon margin, where the commune of Marendo stands. This is treated separately because the threshold moves as the barrier does, and it is revised twice a year on survey.

How a warning is produced

Method by kind of flood
Kind Lead time Inputs Test applied
Flash 30 to 90 min 1 km radar nowcast, soil moisture, drainage capacity of the settlement Rainfall rate over the catchment against the capacity, adjusted for current wetness
Riverine 6 to 48 h Rainfall observed and forecast, snowmelt, soil moisture, upstream gauges Rainfall-runoff model producing stage at each gauged reach, against three fixed levels
Coastal surge 12 to 36 h Pressure and wind forecast, astronomical tide, tide gauge record Surge model over the shelf; total water level against the defended level of each coastal cell
Lagoon overtopping 12 to 36 h Surge output, tide, current barrier crest survey Total water level against the surveyed crest, with a margin left above it

The rainfall-runoff model runs by watershed. Article 28 (2) makes the watershed the unit of water administration and gives its boundaries precedence over the sub-regional map, so a warning follows the basin and crosses administrative lines where the basin does. Where a basin spans a boundary, the warning goes to the joint body of the councils concerned as well as to the public.

Warning steps

Steps and what each states
Step Condition Statement and action
Watch Threshold reachable on the forecast Published to the commune, the basin body and route operators; no public broadcast
Warning First level exceeded or expected Broadcast to the reach or cell. Water expected out of channel; move vehicles and stock; avoid the floodplain and the fords
Severe Second level exceeded or expected Broadcast with the action stated. Danger to life; move to higher ground; ground floors evacuated by the commune
Recession Stage falling below the first level Broadcast stating that water is falling and that the ground remains saturated

The recession step exists because a large share of flood injury occurs after the peak, when people return to a floodplain that is still moving water.

The 2017 event and what followed

Sornorum, July 2017

Two convective systems crossed the southern crossroads within thirty hours and delivered 191 mm at the Sornorum station, of which 106 mm fell in four hours. The drainage of the town had been designed against a one-hour intensity of 26 mm.

Three findings followed. Radar coverage over the southern sub-regions was too coarse to resolve either system in time. No arrangement existed for converting a rainfall observation into a warning to residents. The design intensity for urban drainage was drawn from a rainfall record already superseded.

The first two findings produced REP-FEW. The third produced the revision of the drainage design intensity in 2019, which raised the southern figure from 26 mm to 37 mm an hour.

Limits

What REP-FEW cannot do

Flash flooding is the least predictable kind and the most dangerous. The warning depends on the radar nowcast, and the four radar sites leave the interior of Halvorum, western Aspreduson and the upper Vandorum valleys with coarse coverage. Six of the seven unwarned events over the period occurred in exactly those places.

The rainfall-runoff model is calibrated on gauged reaches. Roughly 3,400 km of watercourse in the Republic is ungauged, mostly small streams on the upland, and stage on those is inferred from the model with no observation to correct it.

Urban surface flooding depends on the state of drains, gullies and screens at the moment of the storm. A blocked gully converts a manageable rainfall into a flooded street, and no instrument reports that condition.

A warning names a reach, a catchment or a coastal cell. Whether a particular building floods depends on ground levels, on defences and on the state of its own drainage, and the system makes no statement at that scale.

Ice affects stage in the continental interior in a hard winter and the model handles it poorly. Warnings during ice conditions on the upper Marnaqui are issued with the qualification stated in the message.
