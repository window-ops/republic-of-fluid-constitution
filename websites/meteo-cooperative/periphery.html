Harm to the periphery

Part of the environmental cost of what the Republic uses falls outside its frontiers. This page states that part in physical quantities: emissions released elsewhere in producing what the Republic imports, together with the water and land used elsewhere for the same purpose. The quantity has fallen in each of the last fourteen years, and the fall tracks the approach to resource independence.

The position being measured

The Republic occupies a semi-peripheral position in the world division of labour. It exports a small number of primary commodities, uranium concentrate above all, and it imports machinery, electronics, specialised chemicals and a number of agricultural goods its own latitude precludes. Exchange of that shape places environmental cost in two directions at once, and the account below concerns the direction that runs outward from the Republic toward the countries producing what it buys.

The terms core, semi-periphery and periphery are used here as a description of trade structure and of nothing else. They are the terms in which the economic material of the Public Register discusses the same position.

Emissions displaced abroad

The consumption account exceeds the territorial account by the emissions released in other countries in producing what the Republic imports. That difference stood at 31 per cent of the territorial figure in 1990 and stands at 14 per cent now. In absolute quantity it has fallen further, since the territorial figure has itself fallen by half over the same period.

Water and land used elsewhere

Two further quantities are estimated on the same basis. Water abstracted abroad in producing imports amounted to 1,840 Gl a year in 1990 and amounts to 490 Gl now. Land occupied abroad for the same purpose amounted to 1.31 million hectares and amounts to 0.44 million. Both series fall faster than the emissions series, since the goods with the largest water and land requirement are the agricultural ones, and those are the imports that domestic production has replaced most completely.

Water abstracted in a basin already in deficit has a weight the aggregate figure conceals. About 61 Gl of the current total is drawn in four basins classed as water-stressed by the producing countries themselves, and that fraction has fallen more slowly than the whole.

What the fall is made of

Four movements account for the decline, and they operate at different speeds.

  • Replacement of imported minerals: The monazite sands at Ravelluri yield thorium and rare earths, and their working since 2004 has displaced the greater part of the rare earth imports on which the electronics of the Republic formerly depended.
  • Secondary recovery: Metals reaching the end of service are recovered under the materials ceiling. Copper, aluminium and steel now come mostly from stock already inside the frontiers.
  • Reduced throughput: The materials ceiling fixed by the Ecological Budget Authority limits the aggregate quantity of material entering the economy each year, so the import series falls with the total.
  • Agricultural substitution: A longer growing season and the reorganisation of the western plain have replaced most imported cereal, oilseed and fibre. This is the movement responsible for the sharp fall in the land and water figures.
Two qualifications

A longer growing season is itself an effect of the warming to which the Republic contributes, so part of the agricultural substitution rests on a change the Republic helped produce.

Resource independence reduces harm placed abroad and leaves the producing countries with the same installations and a smaller buyer. The environmental effect of a purchase ending is measured here; the economic effect on the producer falls outside the mandate and is examined by the External Currency Translation Cooperative.

What does not fall

Electronics and instruments account for the largest remaining item and have fallen the least. Fabrication of semiconductors at current densities lies beyond the industrial capacity of the Republic, and every projection available to the cooperative shows this item persisting at roughly its present size through the 2030s.

Waste is a separate matter and appears nowhere in these tables. Export of waste for treatment or disposal outside the frontiers is prohibited, and no quantity has left the Republic on that footing since 1991. Residues from the mill at Ferrista and from the Ravelluri sands are held on site under the custody obligations that attach to them.

Method and its limits

Uncertainty

Territorial emissions are measured. Embodied emissions are estimated, from the physical quantity imported and from intensity figures the producing countries publish. Where a producer publishes no intensity, a regional mean is applied and the substitution is noted in the dataset.

Uncertainty on the embodied series is roughly plus or minus 18 per cent, against roughly plus or minus 4 per cent on the territorial series. Comparisons between years within the embodied series are more reliable than the level of any single year, since the same method applies throughout.

The series counts environmental quantities. Labour conditions, land tenure and the distribution of the proceeds in the producing countries fall outside it, and the cooperative makes no claim about them.
