About the cooperative

Meteorological Cooperative

Meteorological Cooperative of the Republic

The cooperative observes the atmosphere, the ground and the water of the Republic, issues the public forecast, maintains the national climate record, and operates the three warning systems for earthquake, landslide and flood. It holds the catalogue of species endemic to the territory and publishes every specification, instrument plan and source repository on which its work depends.

StandingCooperative holding a public mandate by organic law
MandateObservation, forecasting, the climate record, seismological and hydrological warning, and the species catalogue
Territory covered230,000 km², two regions, twenty sub-regions, the territorial sea and the Ravelluri lagoon
Climate zoneTemperate transitional, maritime in the north and west, continental in tendency inland and in the east
Warning systemsREP-KJS since 2021, REP-LEW since 2024, REP-FEW since 2025
Intensity scaleREP 1 to 10, derived from the Japanese shindo scale, itself derived from Mercalli
PublicationEvery specification, plan, dataset and repository is public, free of charge and open to forking

What the cooperative does

Four functions fall under the mandate. The first is observation: a network of surface, upper-air, marine, seismic and hydrometric stations reporting continuously into a single archive. The second is forecasting, from the nowcast at fifteen minutes to the seasonal outlook. The third is the climate record, which supplies the physical series the Ecological Budget Authority works from when it fixes the ceilings for a period. The fourth is warning, which covers the three hazards for which seconds or hours of notice change the outcome.

Data collection is shared. Emissions inventories, water quality series and habitat records are gathered jointly with the ecological organs, and the cooperative publishes the physical measurements while the Authority publishes the ceilings drawn from them. Neither body audits the other. Both publish the raw series.

Observed change

Warming of about 1.4 degrees since 1960, the retreat of the eastern shore, the Quintorum harvest failures, and rainfall that has grown more intense without growing more plentiful.

Standing notice on the warning systems

Limits of a warning

An earthquake warning arrives ahead of the shaking because the detection network is quicker than the ground. Sites near the epicentre receive the warning during the shaking or after it, and this blind zone is a property of the physics and not a fault of the equipment.

Landslide and flood warnings rest on rainfall, saturation and river-stage thresholds, and a threshold is a statement about probability. A warning may be issued where no event follows, and an event may occur where no warning was issued.

Publication and forking

REP-KJS, REP-LEW, REP-FEW and the RQUAK client are published in full: source, wire format, conformance vectors, station firmware and the mechanical drawings of the sensor housings. Any country, cooperative or individual may take the whole of it, adapt it and operate it. The cooperative asks for notice of a fork so that the porting guide can record where the systems run, and it imposes no further condition.
