Regulations

The register lists every regulation adopted for the execution of a law, with the law it executes and its present standing.

Article 153(2): a committee adopts regulations for the execution of laws within its field, within their limits and for their purposes alone.

The annulled regulation

The regulation on communal record systems was annulled by the Constitutional Court on 18 March 2026 for exceeding the law it executed. Such a regulation is void, and the annulment was sought by a resident without fee.

The Law on communal records itself stands separately declared incompatible in one section, with a cure period running. The register of cure periods states its position.

The withdrawn regulation

The regulation on the allocation of training places relied upon a scoring system that the data division of the Defender of Rights ordered to cease on 2 February 2026, as scoring of persons for an allocation within Article 51(7). The committee withdrew the regulation, and places are now allocated by a person applying published criteria.

The order and the system are recorded on the register of automated systems.

Publication

Regulations are published in full, in open and machine-readable form, free of charge, with the law each executes stated on its face. The systems administering them run on free and open software whose source is public.
